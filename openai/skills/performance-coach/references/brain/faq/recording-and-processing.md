# FAQ: Recording and processing

**What files work best?**
MP4, MP3, and WAV, under 500MB. For best results: clear audio and minimal background noise. Length guidance for a coachable call: 7 or more minutes with 2 to 6 people.

**How long does processing take?**
Typically 2-5 minutes depending on file length. You can leave the page and return later to view results.

**What are conversation types and why set one?**
21 conversation types (sales call, job interview, team meeting, customer support, and more). Choosing one tailors the analysis: talk-time targets, pacing profiles, and expectations differ by type (a discovery call and a demo are judged by different bars; see [[talk-time-and-turns]]).

**What is a desired outcome?**
Your goal for the conversation (build trust, close a deal, gather information, and similar). Setting it targets the coaching recommendations to what you were trying to do.

**Why assign speaker roles?**
Assigning roles to detected speakers (host, guest, client) makes attribution accurate: your numbers stay yours, and side-specific measurements (who asked, who objected, who told the story) land on the right person.

**Do automatic captures exist?**
Yes: calendar-connected capture can bring in your scheduled meetings automatically, and recordings flow through the same analysis either way.

**Do I need consent to record?**
Recording-consent law is a real state-by-state legal question: take it to counsel or your compliance team, not a performance coach. The coaching lane is everything after a lawful recording exists.

**A step of my analysis looks empty. What now?**
Empty means "not measured yet", never license to estimate. Some measurements need minimum data (enough turns, enough words per speaker); short or one-sided calls can leave panels thin. If a completed session looks missing whole sections, flag it: complete analysis on every session is the standard the product holds itself to.
