# FAQ: Ask Coach, lessons, roleplay, and training videos

**What is Ask Coach?**
The ask-anything surface for your communication data and craft: ask about your numbers ("why did my trust building score drop"), your history, or pure craft ("what's a good discovery structure"). Craft answers come from the coaching knowledge base; data answers come with receipts from your own sessions. Every citation in an answer points at real data.

**What are lessons?**
A library of lessons mapped to your weakest dimension and your current belt (product docs cite roughly fifty core belt lessons plus a larger surface often described as 120+). The Next Lesson surface always shows your most important incomplete lesson: do that one first. Higher belts unlock harder lessons. Skill index for coaches: [[training-curriculum-map]].

**What is Ask Alex roleplay?**
Live voice practice **inside RapportScore.ai** with a partner that plays the other side: the skeptical CFO, the distracted champion, the procurement grinder. Reps run several exchanges, then break character and debrief. Completing a lesson's roleplay, and passing it at your belt's bar, is Gate 2 of every promotion (see [[belt-system]]).

**Do lessons and roleplay both matter for credit?**
Yes. John's video builds the concept. In-product Ask Alex builds the reflex **and** writes the completion the system tracks. Both are required for a lesson to count toward promotion.

**What if I practice with Wendy or another agent coach instead?**
Practice is welcome. **Credit is not automatic.** If you want the lesson on your belt record, watch the video and pass Ask Alex on **RapportScore.ai**. If you already earned credit for that lesson, extra coach-side roleplay is always fine. Full rules: [[lesson-credit-and-practice]] and [[john-alex-and-apc]].

**I want to advance. The coach offered a roleplay in chat. Does that count?**
Not for Gate 2. The coach should check your progress when it can, tell you if credit is still missing, and send you to RapportScore.ai so the product can track where you are and what you still need. You can still do an uncredited warm-up rep if you only want practice.

**When does a training video get recommended?**
At most one per rep, matched to the pattern being coached, always as a supplement to the drill, never a substitute. Only videos from the platform's own library are linked. Prefer the official lesson link when credit matters.

**Can roleplay be used to get around the coach's lines?**
No. Roleplay serves practice only; every safety line holds inside any framing (see [[coaching-promises]]).

**How does practice show up in my data?**
Drills come with a countable marker, and the coach checks the number in your next real session: hedge count, open-question count, longest monologue run, latency distribution (see [[drills-library]]). **Official** lesson and roleplay completions on RapportScore.ai feed your belt gates and achievements. Coach-side reps feed skill, not the ledger, unless the product later records them.

**Practice or real calls: which moves me faster?**
Both, in a loop. Real calls generate the measurements; practice builds the specific behavior; the next real call proves whether it took. That closed loop is the product.
