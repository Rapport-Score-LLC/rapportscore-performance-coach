# Active Listening

One of the seven Rapport Dimensions. Active Listening is measured algorithmically from response timing, acknowledgment words, clarifying questions, and paraphrasing. It shows how well you are truly hearing the other person, as behavior the transcript can verify.

## Why it matters

People decide within minutes whether you are tracking them. Proof of listening builds trust faster than any pitch.

## The observable behavior

Summarize what they said in one sentence before you respond.

## Transfer plan (from your coaching path)

- **Do**: reflect back one key phrase they used, then pause.
- **When**: right after they explain a problem or concern.
- **Proof**: your Active Listening score rises on your next analyzed call.

## How to read your score

Scored 0-100 on the Trust and Rapport radar; gates belts at the shared bar (see [[belt-system]]). The related measurements below are the observable substrate the product tracks.

## Related measurements

- [[backchannel]]: the short "mm-hmm" and "right" tokens that signal attention without taking the floor.
- [[embedded-acknowledgments]]: acknowledgment plus content in one move ("yeah, and that's exactly the problem").
- [[miti-classification]]: reflections, including the simple-versus-complex distinction (companion key `reflective_listening`).
- [[complex-reflections]]: paraphrase with added meaning, the strongest listening move the system detects.
- [[response-latency]]: timing that shows you processed before replying.

## Drills

- One-line reflection: before responding to any explained problem, reflect one key phrase of theirs, then pause. See [[drills-library]].
- The cost question: when you hear a pain word, ask what it cost them before you explain anything.

## FAQ

**Do backchannels count as filler words?**
No. Backchannel utterances are excluded from filler counts by design, so active listening never inflates your disfluency numbers.

**I listen carefully but score low. Why?**
The measurement can only see verbal evidence: acknowledgments, reflections, timing, clarifying questions. Silent comprehension is real listening but invisible listening. The transfer plan above makes it visible.
