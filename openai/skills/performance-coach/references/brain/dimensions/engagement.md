# Engagement

One of the seven Rapport Dimensions. Engagement measures the energy and participation you bring: whether the conversation is a live two-way exchange or a flat delivery.

## Why it matters

Energy is contagious. Flat delivery loses the room long before your content does.

## The observable behavior

Vary your pace and check in with a question every few minutes.

## Transfer plan (from your coaching path)

- **Do**: break long explanations with a check-in question.
- **When**: any time you have been talking for more than a minute.
- **Proof**: your Engagement score rises on your next analyzed call.

## How to read your score

Scored 0-100 on the Trust and Rapport radar; gates belts at the shared bar (see [[belt-system]]). Engagement's observable substrate is participation shape: turn balance, monologue length, question cadence, and energy signals across the call.

## Related measurements

- [[talk-time-and-turns]]: turn counts and talk share; long unbroken runs are the classic engagement drain.
- [[timing-critical-moments]]: flags long monologues on your side (90-second floor) and, positively, sustained customer stories on theirs.
- [[speech-rate-modulation]]: converging pace is an engagement signal; a final-third acceleration run often marks pressure.
- [[backchannel]]: your listener-side engagement when the other person holds the floor.

## Drills

- Land-and-hand: when you feel a long run starting, land the point in one sentence and hand it back with an open question. See [[drills-library]].
- The check-in cadence: one question for every minute of explanation.

## FAQ

**Is high energy always good?**
Matched energy is good. Running high while the other person stays flat can overwhelm; the Emotional Timeline view shows whether your activation levels tracked each other.

**They talked for ten straight minutes. Is my engagement low?**
Not necessarily. A customer sustaining the floor for 120 seconds or more is detected as a positive engagement signal (customer story, see [[timing-critical-moments]]). Engagement is about keeping the exchange alive, and sometimes the most engaged move is staying out of the way.
