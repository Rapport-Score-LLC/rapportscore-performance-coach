# Brown Belt

Expert-level ability. At Brown you coach yourself effectively between sessions and your metrics show sustained high performance. The bar you hold: every dimension at 85 or better.

## How Brown was earned (the three gates)

1. **Score**: all seven Rapport Dimensions averaged at least 85 over recent completed calls.
2. **Training**: every Purple Belt lesson completed with a passed Ask Alex roleplay.
3. **Consistency**: at least 8 completed calls since the Purple promotion, with the last 4 consecutive calls clearing every bar at 85.

## The path to Black

1. **Score**: all seven dimensions average at least 89.
2. **Training**: every Brown Belt lesson completed with a passed roleplay (Brown lessons pass at the Brown bar of 85).
3. **Consistency**: at least 10 completed calls since your Brown promotion, and your last 5 consecutive calls each clear every dimension at 89.

Promotion is automatic when all three gates pass. See [[belt-system]].

## Coaching focus at Brown

- The gap from 85 to 89 is small in points and large in discipline: it is usually one recurring behavior under one recurring condition, visible only against your own baselines ([[personal-baselines]]).
- Brown-stage coaching often runs at the High Performance dial level: streaks tracked, gaps stated against your own stated standard (see [[intensity-dial]]).
- Self-coaching between sessions is the Brown skill: review your own tape, set one intention per call, verify it in the next analysis.

## FAQ

**Ten calls feels like a lot. Why?**
Black is the certification that your level is who you are on calls, not a streak of good days. Ten completed calls since Brown, with the last five consecutive all clearing 89 everywhere, is what makes the final belt mean something.

**What typically gates people here?**
Whichever dimension degrades under pressure. Common Brown-to-Black work: conviction language at commitment points ([[clean-commitments]]) and regulation during friction ([[emotional-regulation]]).
