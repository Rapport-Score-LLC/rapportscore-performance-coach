# Blue Belt

Developing advanced skills. At Blue you are actively working rapport-building techniques and seeing score improvements across sessions. The bar you hold: every dimension at 65 or better.

## How Blue was earned (the three gates)

1. **Score**: all seven Rapport Dimensions averaged at least 65 over recent completed calls.
2. **Training**: every Yellow Belt lesson completed with a passed Ask Alex roleplay.
3. **Consistency**: at least 6 completed calls since the Yellow promotion, with the last 3 consecutive calls clearing every bar at 65.

## The path to Purple

1. **Score**: all seven dimensions average at least 78.
2. **Training**: every Blue Belt lesson completed with a passed roleplay (Blue lessons pass at the Blue bar of 65).
3. **Consistency**: at least 6 completed calls since your Blue promotion, and your last 3 consecutive calls each clear every dimension at 78.

Promotion is automatic when all three gates pass. See [[belt-system]].

## Coaching focus at Blue

- The jump from 65 to 78 is the steepest proportional climb in the ladder. Plateaus are normal here; they are broken dimension by dimension, not by general effort.
- Blue-stage work usually shifts from mechanics to reading the other side: acknowledgment quality ([[emotional-acknowledgment]]), style matching ([[language-style-matching]]), recovery after friction ([[recovery-episodes]]).
- Keep the streak in view: a single non-qualifying call restarts the 3-call streak toward Purple.

## FAQ

**I cleared 78 once. Why no promotion?**
The streak gate needs your last 3 consecutive calls all clearing 78 on every dimension, plus the call minimum and completed Blue lessons. Consistency is the point: the belt certifies a level you hold, not a level you touched.

**Is 78 on one call type enough?**
Qualifying calls are your completed analyzed sessions since promotion, whatever their type. Comparing like-for-like (discovery against discovery) is still the fairest way to read your own trend.
