# White Belt

Everyone starts here. White Belt is the awareness stage: learning to recognize your communication patterns and understanding the seven Rapport Dimensions. There is no entry requirement and no score bar to hold; the work is recording real calls and getting your first honest measurements.

## What this belt is about

- Getting your baseline: your first analyzed sessions and your Quiz baseline establish where you actually are, not where you guess you are.
- Learning the seven dimensions: [[responsiveness]], [[active-listening]], [[mirroring]], [[emotional-acknowledgment]], [[curiosity]], [[trust-building]], [[engagement]].
- Building the recording habit: regular sessions give the system data to coach you with.

## The path to Yellow (the three gates)

1. **Score**: all seven dimensions average at least 50 over your recent completed calls.
2. **Training**: every White Belt lesson completed with a passed Ask Alex roleplay (White Belt lessons pass at a floor of 50).
3. **Consistency**: at least 4 completed calls since starting, and your last 2 consecutive calls each clear every dimension bar.

Promotion happens automatically when all three pass. See [[belt-system]] for the full model.

## Coaching focus at White

- One pattern at a time. The coach will pick your single highest-leverage behavior and park the rest.
- Expect "worth watching" language, not "pattern" language: patterns need evidence across 3 or more sessions, and you are still building history.
- Fastest first wins are usually mechanical: response timing, question count, talk-time balance.

## FAQ

**How many calls do I need before coaching gets specific?**
The first analyzed call already produces real measurements. Personal baselines firm up as history grows: under 4 sessions the system leans on population norms, and by 10 or more your baselines are fully your own (see [[personal-baselines]]).

**What should I record?**
A real conversation, 7 or more minutes, 2 to 6 people. See [[getting-started]] and [[recording-and-processing]].
