# Trust Indicators

## What it measures

Linguistic trust signals scored 0 to 100 with a trajectory (building, eroding, or insufficient_data). It measures commitment language, appropriate vulnerability, and consistency of promises across the call: the observable language behaviors associated with trust, never a mind-reading claim. Research anchors: function-word psychology (Tausczik and Pennebaker 2010) and self-disclosure and liking research (Collins and Miller 1994).

## Where you see it

The Trust tile on the Signal Stack (Deep Dive tab), and trust trajectory context inside Ask Coach answers about trust language.

## How to read your number

- Direction: higher is stronger trust language; the trajectory (building versus eroding) matters as much as the level.
- The product is candid that this signal is exploratory: the underlying lexicon is under research and current scores run low across all sessions. Compare against your own history, not an absolute bar. This is why the note carries expert-estimate provenance for its numbers.
- Combined reads from the card: high reciprocity with low trust reads as engaged but skeptical; low reciprocity with building trust reads as warming up but not yet active (see [[counterpart-reciprocity]]).
- "Insufficient_data" means the call had too little trust-relevant language to score; it is not a low score.

## What moves it

- Commitment language kept clean: owner plus action plus date, without escape hatches (see [[clean-commitments]]). Hedged promises are the fastest drag on trust language.
- Consistency of promises: saying the same thing about the same commitment across the call.
- Appropriate openness: volunteering a real limitation or trade-off before being asked (the [[trust-building]] transfer plan).
- Truthful risk-reversal where it is real: language that makes yes safe.

## Drills

- One clean commitment per call, verified in the next session's data. See [[drills-library]].
- Volunteer the trade-off: one honest limitation named plainly when fit or risk comes up.

## FAQ

**My trust score is low but the meeting felt great. Which is right?**
Possibly both. The signal reads specific language behaviors and is known to score conservatively while the lexicon is under research. Use the trajectory and your own trend; bring the discrepancy to the coach and it will show you exactly which language it counted.

**Can I game it with trust-sounding words?**
Performing the tokens without the behavior polishes the mirror instead of the face. The durable lever is the behavior the words describe: commitments made cleanly and kept. See [[determinism-and-gaming]].
