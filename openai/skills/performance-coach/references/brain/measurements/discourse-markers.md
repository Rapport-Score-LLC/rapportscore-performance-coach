# Discourse Markers

## What it measures

The certainty profile of your communication across the session, read from discourse markers: the small words and phrases that signal how confidently something is said (hedges like "maybe" and "I think", boosters like "definitely", softeners, connectives). The headline output is a profile label for the focus speaker: highly_certain, balanced, or hedging. Research anchor: a composite of politeness and face-work research across six of the seven marker categories (including Park 2008 on linguistic politeness and face-work).

## Where you see it

The Discourse tile on the Signal Stack (Deep Dive tab). Related hedge behavior also appears in the entrainment hedge-convergence chart and in commitment coaching.

## How to read your number

- No numeric threshold is published for the profile; it is a categorical read.
- Neither pole is automatically good. Highly_certain fits recommendations and closing moments; balanced fits most consultative work; a hedging profile in a call where you were the expert usually signals under-claimed authority.
- The high-stakes read: where the hedging sits matters more than the total. Hedges inside pricing sentences and commitment moments carry the cost (see [[clean-commitments]]).
- Context check: hedging an estimate is honesty ("that's probably a caching issue"); hedging an owned future action is the pattern ("I'll probably get it done"). The coach applies this distinction before naming anything.

## What moves it

- Deciding before speaking: certainty language follows actual decisions.
- The naked number: stating price or timeline once without softeners ("it's just, basically, around 40k, kind of depending" versus the real sentence).
- Keeping honest uncertainty clean: "I don't know yet; I'll confirm by Friday" is a certain sentence about an uncertain thing.

## Drills

- One naked number per call: price or timeline stated once with no softener attached. See [[drills-library]].
- Clean conditional practice: convert one vague promise into either a clean commitment or a clean conditional.

## FAQ

**Is hedging always weakness?**
No. Epistemic hedging is honesty about estimates, and some softening is personal voice. The coached pattern is hedging your own commitments and expertise, especially at high-stakes moments.

**Can my profile change within one call?**
The tile is a session-level profile, but the underlying markers are per-utterance; the entrainment view shows hedge rates per call third, which is where within-call drift becomes visible.
