# Score Bands: how to read a 0-100 score

## What it measures

The normative interpretation layer: how a 0-100 score translates into a performance band when the product narrates results. The methodology defines a five-level performance band; the two levels documented in this vault's sources are **Exceptional at 80 and above** and **Good at 65 and above**, with Average and the remaining levels below (the exact lower cutoffs live in the product methodology document and are logged as a gap in this vault's ledger, never guessed here).

## Where you see it

Narrated interpretations in reports and coaching summaries, and echoed in product guidance: the rapport tutorial says "aim for 65+ for good rapport, 80+ for exceptional", and the Performance Dashboard marks rapport 70+ as a strong connection.

## How to read your number

- 0-100 scales run through the product: overall rapport, each of the seven dimensions, and many composite scores.
- 80 and above: exceptional territory. 65 and above: good. Below that, the score is telling you where the growth is, which is the product working as designed.
- Bands describe a session's measured behavior, never a person's worth or overall job performance.
- Belt bars are a separate, stricter ladder on the same scale: every dimension must hold 50 / 65 / 78 / 85 / 89 for Yellow through Black (see [[belt-system]]). A "Good" 65 everywhere is exactly the Blue Belt bar; Black demands 89 everywhere, well inside the Exceptional band.
- Score displays are belt-aware (compression by level), so early-stage progress stays visible; the bands above are about the underlying 0-100 scale.

## What moves it

- Whatever moves the underlying measurement: bands are interpretation, not a separate score. Improve the dimension, and the band follows.

## Drills

- Band-to-behavior translation: when a report names a band, ask the coach which single measurement most cheaply moves it, then run that measurement's drill. See [[drills-library]].

## FAQ

**Is 72 good?**
By the documented bands, 72 sits above Good (65) and below Exceptional (80). Against belts, 72 clears the Blue bar (65) and not the Purple bar (78). Both readings are true; which matters depends on whether you are reading a session or chasing a promotion.

**Why do different cards state slightly different "strong" numbers?**
Some cards carry their own guidance (70+ strong on the dashboard) alongside the methodology bands (65 Good, 80 Exceptional). The variance is logged for reconciliation; when in doubt, the methodology bands are the narrated standard.
