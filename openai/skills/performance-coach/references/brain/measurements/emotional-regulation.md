# Emotional Regulation Under Friction

## What it measures

How consistently you maintained your communication style during challenging moments: objections, extended silences, disagreements, and pressure points. The verdict is a regulation band (HIGH, MEDIUM, or LOW) built from countable metrics: Friction Events (total friction moments detected), Repair Rate (share of friction events followed by an acknowledgment, clarifying question, paraphrase, or forward action), De-escalation Rate (share with a repair response and no escalation), and Escalation Rate (share where the conversation became more tense after the trigger). Research anchor: the process model of emotion regulation (Gross 1998).

## Where you see it

The Emotional Regulation card: band, the four metrics, behavioral evidence, a recovery-pattern summary in the expanded view, and the session's regulation band echoed on the Session Safety Contribution card.

## How to read your number

- HIGH regulation does not mean robotic: it means you adjusted intentionally rather than reactively.
- A low De-escalation Rate with a moderate Repair Rate means you tried to recover but did not fully settle the tension: practice naming the tension before responding.
- A high Escalation Rate with a low Repair Rate is the most coachable pattern: friction went unaddressed and intensified.
- The Recovery Pattern text describes your typical arc after friction: quick recovery versus staying off-balance for multiple turns.
- Confidence note: without voice activity detection, overlap-based friction has low reliability; the card labels this "(ASR-only)" and leans on language and timing signals.

## What moves it

- Behavior under pressure is the entire measurement: what your language does in the two minutes after a hard moment.
- Style drift under friction (speeding up, shortening sentences, hedging more) is the signature counterparts detect; holding pace and syntax steady reads as composure.
- Naming tension out loud ("I hear that this part is frustrating") is a repair move that also de-escalates.
- Regulated speakers are described in the product as being perceived as more trustworthy and competent by counterparts.

## Drills

- Objection roleplay is the fastest way to raise De-escalation Rate across sessions: practice pause, restate, hold with Ask Alex. See [[drills-library]] and [[ask-coach-and-training]].
- Behavioral-evidence review: read the card's evidence list after each session; those are your specific moments, not generalizations.

## FAQ

**Does a LOW band mean I got emotional?**
It means your observable style shifted under pressure. The measurement never claims to know what you felt; it counts what your language and timing did.

**Friction Events was high. Bad call?**
Not by itself. Hard conversations have friction. The graded part is repair and de-escalation: what happened next.
