# Agency Orientation

## What it measures

Whether you drove the conversation or primarily reacted to it. The verdict is a band (PROACTIVE, BALANCED, or REACTIVE) built from four percentage metrics: Turn Initiation% (share of your turns started after a meaningful gap plus speaker change), Topic Intro Rate% (share of topic changes you initiated), Question Dominance% (of the question exchanges you were part of, the share where you asked rather than answered: asked divided by asked plus answered), and Reframe Rate% (reframes after objections divided by total objections). Research anchors: social cognitive theory of agency (Bandura 2001) and self-determination theory (Deci and Ryan 2000).

## Where you see it

The Agency Orientation card (v1.1), with the band, four metric tiles, behavioral evidence, a typical stall pattern box when a recurring pattern exists, and a coaching implication box.

## How to read your number

- The right band depends on context: proactive fits sales and coaching (you set the agenda, steer topics, ask more than you answer); reactive is appropriate in support calls but is a gap in discovery or negotiation.
- Question Dominance%: 100% means you only asked and never answered; 0% means you only answered. A low value in a discovery call means you mostly answered questions, which usually signals explaining rather than exploring.
- Confidence label: when voice activity detection is unavailable, the label appends "(ASR-only)" because interrupt-recovery signals have low reliability without it; the analysis then leans on turn-taking and question patterns.
- The Behavioral Evidence list contains the specific moments that anchored your band: start there, not with the headline.

## What moves it

- Turn initiation: leading with a question or observation at the start of your next few turns.
- Topic introduction: introducing a new topic after every two exchanges when you have been following their agenda.
- Reframes: naming an objection, validating it, then steering to a new angle before answering (see [[objection-structure]]).
- Question dominance: one asked question before every answered one in discovery.

## Drills

- Recommendation-first: answer one "what do you think?" with a position and one reason before offering alternatives. See [[drills-library]].
- Topic-intro cadence: one deliberate topic introduction per ten minutes of call.

## FAQ

**Is REACTIVE bad?**
Only against the wrong call type. A support call run reactively is done right. The card reads your posture against what the conversation called for.

**My band surprised me. Where do I check?**
The Behavioral Evidence list. It cites the actual moments, so you can verify the classification against the transcript rather than arguing with the label.
