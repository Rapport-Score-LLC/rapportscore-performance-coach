# Objections and Responses

## What it measures

The objections raised during a call and how effectively they were addressed: each objection's type and severity, your response to it, and the resolution effectiveness. Speaker Statistics also counts Objections Raised and Responses Given per speaker. Research anchor: dialogue-act objection typology (ISO 24617-2 dialogue-act annotation standard) with sales-conversation research.

## Where you see it

The Objections and Responses card (Detailed tab), objection counts in the Performance Dashboard and Speaker Statistics, shield pins on the Communication Timeline, and post-objection silence events in [[timing-critical-moments]].

## How to read your number

- No universal "good" objection count: objections often mean engagement. The read is the handling, not the raw total.
- The card's handling framework: Acknowledge (validate the concern), Clarify (understand the real issue), Respond (address with evidence and logic), Confirm (check whether it resolved).
- Common patterns from the card: price objections often mask other concerns; timing objections need urgency grounded in the real cost of waiting; authority objections need stakeholder mapping. Track which objection types you handle best across sessions.
- The wobble read (coaching pattern, within-session baseline): a spike in your hedges and softeners, or an unprompted concession, inside the two minutes after an objection cue is conviction giving ground on contact. A held pause followed by a held position is the healthy opposite.

## What moves it

- Acknowledging before answering: validation first earns the right to respond (see [[emotional-acknowledgment]]).
- Clarifying the real issue instead of rebutting the surface words.
- Holding your position for one full turn before offering anything: an unasked discount 40 seconds after "that's steep" teaches the buyer that pushing works.
- Falsifiers the coach applies: a concession you planned and initiated before any objection is strategy, not wobble; objection language you quoted or raised yourself does not count.

## Drills

- Pause, restate, hold: at the next objection, pause 1.5-2.0 seconds, restate their concern in one line, hold your position for one full turn before offering anything. Verify hedge density in objection windows next session. See [[drills-library]].
- Reframe rep: name the objection, validate it, steer to a new angle, then answer (raises Reframe Rate in [[agency-orientation]]).

## FAQ

**Are objections a bad sign?**
Usually the opposite: silence is how deals die quietly. An objection is engagement plus information. What the measurement grades is what happened next.

**What if the objection is right?**
Agree with it honestly. Volunteering a real limitation is measured trust-building behavior (see [[trust-building]]), and truthful handling always beats a clever dodge.
