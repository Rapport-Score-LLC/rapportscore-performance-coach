# Coach Read: Response Latency

Product measurement note: [[response-latency]]

## What this measures

The millisecond gap between when the partner finishes and when this person begins their response, averaged across the call. The cross-cultural baseline is around 200 milliseconds, which is faster than reaction time and means the response is being planned before the partner finishes speaking.

## What a high score means

In this measurement, "high" means a long latency. A long latency is not automatically a problem. The signal worth reading is the variation. A team member with appropriately long latencies after complex or emotional content, and short latencies after social or simple exchanges, is showing context-sensitive responding. The coaching opportunity surfaces only when latency is uniformly long (suggesting withdrawn participation) or when it suddenly spikes mid-call (suggesting a topic just landed harder than expected).

## What a low score means

A low latency, near the 200-300 millisecond range, signals engagement and anticipatory listening: this team member is tracking the partner's turn closely enough to plan the response in real time. Team contribution: their conversations feel quick and alive, especially in dyadic work.

## How the report uses this

Response Latency appears in Section 4 (Communication Dynamics) and feeds Responsiveness in Section 5. The convergence between the two speakers' latency patterns is reported alongside, because mutual convergence is a stronger predictor of social outcomes than absolute latency.
