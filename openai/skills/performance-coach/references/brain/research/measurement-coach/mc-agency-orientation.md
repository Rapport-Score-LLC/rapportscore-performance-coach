# Coach Read: Agency Orientation

Product measurement note: [[agency-orientation]]

## What this measures

Who steers the conversation, when, and how the steering hand changes across the call.

## What a high score means

A high agency score means this team member is comfortable taking the wheel. They set agendas, name transitions, and ask the question that resets the frame. The team contribution is structural: they move conversations from open exploration into committed next steps.

## What a low score means

A low agency score means this team member lets the conversation come to them. They yield the floor, follow rather than lead, and ask questions that hand control back. That can be exactly right in discovery and coaching, where the goal is to draw the counterpart out. The coaching opportunity is to help them recognize the moment when the room is asking them to take a turn, and to practice the small phrases that claim it without seizing it.

## How the report uses this

Agency Orientation surfaces in Section 4 (Communication Dynamics) alongside Turn-Taking and Topic Control. Look at whether the agency profile matches the role they hold and the phase of work they are leading.
