# Coach Read: Interruptions

Product measurement note: [[interruptions]]

## What this measures

How often this person speaks over a partner, and whether the overlap is the cooperative kind (finishing thoughts, agreeing audibly) or the competitive kind (cutting off, redirecting).

## What a high score means

A high interruption count is not automatically a problem. Read the cooperative-to-competitive ratio next to the raw count. A high count with a high cooperative ratio is a high-involvement style: this team member overlaps to signal enthusiasm, completion, and agreement, and partners with the same style read it as warmth. Team contribution in fast, energetic rooms is momentum.

## What a low score means

A low count signals a team member who waits cleanly for turn boundaries. In considerate-style cultures and one-on-one coaching, this reads as respect. The coaching opportunity surfaces only if the count is so low they never claim the floor when the conversation needs them to: at that point the practice is to lean into projected transition points rather than waiting for explicit silence.

## How the report uses this

Interruptions appears in Section 4 (Communication Dynamics) and is one of the strongest inputs into Equity in Section 5. Read the cooperative-to-competitive ratio, not the raw count alone, and look at it next to Topic Control to see whether overlaps are about energy or about taking the floor.
