# Coach Read: Question Sequencing

Product measurement note: [[questions-asked]]

## What this measures

How this person's questions chain together: whether each question references the prior answer (catalytic) or stands alone as a new topic (cathartic), whether the depth of questioning rises through discovery, and how coherently the line of inquiry holds.

## What a high score means

A high score means follow-up questions are doing most of the work. This team member's third question explicitly builds on what the partner said in answer to the first and second, which is the pattern Yeomans and colleagues showed compounds in disclosure and likability. Team contribution is depth: they get past the surface story to the underlying interest, and partners feel listened to as they do it.

## What a low score means

A low score usually means questions are coming off a mental checklist rather than off the partner's last answer. Topics scatter, depth never accumulates, and high-disclosure moments get missed because the follow-up never lands. The coaching opportunity is the simplest move in the discipline: catch the next high-disclosure answer and stay on it for one more question instead of moving on.

## How the report uses this

Question Sequencing appears in Section 4 (Communication Dynamics) alongside Question Structure. Structure tells you what kinds of questions; sequencing tells you whether they form a coherent line of inquiry or a sequence of independent probes.
