# Coach Read: Rapport Moments

Product measurement note: [[recovery-episodes]]

## What this measures

Discrete in-call events where rapport visibly moved: active listening, alignment shifts, commitments, great questions, interruptions, objection handling, and rapport risks. Each moment is anchored to a timestamp in the transcript with an impact score and a band (Low, Medium, High) computed against the person's own distribution.

## What a high score means

A high count of High-band moments, especially across multiple moment types, indicates a team member who creates and recognizes pivot points in conversations. They land great questions, handle objections cleanly, and make commitments the other side reciprocates. Team contribution: their calls are worth reviewing turn-by-turn, because the moments will teach.

## What a low score means

A low count, or a heavy lean into rapport-risk moments above the user's own P75, signals that the call generated more friction than connection. The coaching opportunity is not aggregate; it is specific. Open the timestamped moments and watch the 60 seconds around each one. The story is in the sequence, not the count.

## How the report uses this

Rapport Moments appears in Section 4 (Communication Dynamics) and feeds the per-user impact bands shown throughout the report. Bands are personalized once the user has 30 or more scored moments; before that, corpus-wide bands are used as a fallback.
