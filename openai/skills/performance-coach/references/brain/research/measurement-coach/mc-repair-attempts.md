# Coach Read: Repair Attempts

Product measurement note: [[repair-attempts]]

## What this measures

The individual moves people make to correct a misunderstanding, clarify a meaning, or recover from a small breakdown: "what I meant was," "let me rephrase," "did you mean," "sorry, I think I lost you."

## What a high score means

A high repair-attempt rate is not a sign of trouble. It is a sign that this team member is actively maintaining mutual understanding rather than letting confusion accumulate. They notice breakdowns within a few seconds and act on them. Team contribution: clarity. Conversations they participate in end with both sides genuinely aligned, not just nodding along.

## What a low score means

A low rate in a substantive conversation usually means breakdowns are going unaddressed. Either they are not being noticed, or they are being noticed and pushed past. The coaching opportunity is to install the three-to-five-second window after any tension marker: that is the window where Gottman's research shows repair has the highest success rate. After that window the breakdown compounds.

## How the report uses this

Repair Attempts appears in Section 4 (Communication Dynamics) and feeds Clarity and Trust in Section 5. Read it as the micro signal next to Recovery Episodes (the macro arc): repair attempts are the building blocks that complete a recovery.
