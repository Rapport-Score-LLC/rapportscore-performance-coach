# Coach Read: Recovery Episodes

Product measurement note: [[recovery-episodes]]

## What this measures

Whole arcs where the conversation hit a rupture (a misunderstanding, an emotional spike, an awkward silence after a hard topic) and then either recovered all the way back to baseline or got stuck partway. The arc has five phases: tension detection, acknowledgment, repair attempt, verification, restoration.

## What a high score means

A high score means this team member completes the arc. They notice tension early, name it rather than minimize it, try a specific repair, check whether the repair landed, and only then move on. Team contribution is durability: their relationships survive disagreement and often deepen because of it, which is Gottman's central finding.

## What a low score means

A low score typically shows up as abandoned arcs: tension gets named, a repair gets attempted, and the conversation pivots to a new topic without verifying the previous one was resolved. The cost is "relationship debt" that accumulates across calls. The coaching opportunity is the verification step. One sentence: "did that land for you?" That single move is what turns a partial recovery into a complete one.

## How the report uses this

Recovery Episodes appears in Section 4 (Communication Dynamics) and is a key input into Trust in Section 5. Read it next to Repair Attempts: Recovery is the macro arc, Repair Attempts is the individual moves inside it.
