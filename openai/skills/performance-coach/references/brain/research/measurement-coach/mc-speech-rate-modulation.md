# Coach Read: Speech Rate Modulation

Product measurement note: [[speech-rate-modulation]]

## What this measures

Words per second across the call for both speakers, with three derived views: how much the two rates converge over time, where sustained accelerations or decelerations cluster, and the overall shape of the call (balanced, primary-led, or mixed).

## What a high score means

A high convergence score in a balanced call means this team member is naturally tuning their pace to the partner's. The research is consistent: dyads whose speech rates converge tend to cooperate more in subsequent tasks. They also slow on moments that need weight and speed up on moments that need energy. Team contribution: they sound like they are in the conversation rather than delivering at it.

## What a low score means

A low convergence score, or a rigidly monotonic pace, often means this team member talks at one speed regardless of context. In demos and presentations that can be fine. In dyadic work it reads as detached or rehearsed. The coaching opportunity is to deliberately slow the pace for one or two emphasis moments per call and notice the partner's response.

## How the report uses this

Speech Rate Modulation appears in Section 4 (Communication Dynamics) and contributes to Mirroring in Section 5. The call-shape classification gates interpretation: convergence in a primary-led call (a demo or presentation) is reporting on delivery, not rapport.
