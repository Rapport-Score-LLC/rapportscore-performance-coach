# Coach Read: Emotional Regulation

Product measurement note: [[emotional-regulation]]

## What this measures

How steady this person stays as the emotional temperature of the conversation rises and falls, and who they tend to lead toward (or follow into) the next emotional register.

## What a high score means

A high score points to a team member who modulates affect to fit the room. They acknowledge a partner's frustration before reframing it, slow their pace under heat instead of accelerating, and tend to drive de-escalation rather than catch the contagion. Team contribution is anchor weight: in conversations with stakes, the room calibrates off their composure.

## What a low score means

A low score usually means this team member is more emotionally expressive (or more emotionally absorbent) than their context rewards. Their sentiment trajectory swings, they catch a partner's escalation, or they flatten affect into a register the partner reads as withdrawn. The coaching opportunity is not to suppress emotion; it is to add named-emotion language ("I can hear this is frustrating") and pace control as the first two moves before any reasoning move.

## How the report uses this

Emotional Regulation appears in Section 4 (Communication Dynamics) and is one of the strongest inputs into Warmth and Trust in Section 5. Read it next to Recovery Episodes to see whether emotional spikes get resolved or only weathered.
