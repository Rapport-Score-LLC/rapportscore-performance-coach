# DISC Style: IS / Influencer-Steady

Session-observed communication style guidance. Not personality destiny. People flex across contexts. Verify across multiple interactions.

## Headline

An IS builds the room and keeps it standing. They are the team member most likely to be the one a long-term customer asks for by name a year after the deal closed.

## In conversation

IS speakers carry an Influencer's opening warmth and an Influencer's lexical entrainment (LSM is among the highest of any DISC blend), but the S-secondary flattens the talk share curve across the meeting. Where a pure I spikes early and tapers, an IS holds steady. Response latency is short but not overlapping; the S-secondary lets the prior speaker land before the IS responds. Question architecture leans open and affiliative throughout, with confirming and clarifying forms appearing more often than a pure I would use ("can you say more about that," "did I hear that right"). Challenge questions are very rare. Interruption profile is almost entirely cooperative, and the rate is lower than a pure I's; the S-secondary suppresses the I's instinct to overlap. Repair attempts are frequent, warm, and the most patient of any DISC blend.

## Team contribution

IS speakers are the team's long-cycle relationship anchor. They are most valuable in account management, renewals, internal change windows, and any conversation where a counterpart needs to feel both welcomed and not rushed. Without an IS, teams with strong I-energy often win new business and lose retention; teams with strong S-energy often retain relationships but fail to expand them.

## Allowable weakness

IS speakers rarely close. The combined I and S signature makes commitment-naming uncomfortable; the conversation feels good and the next step is left implicit. They can also absorb friction from both sides of a difficult conversation rather than surface it, leaving themselves carrying weight that should have been redistributed.

## Watch for

- An IS whose meetings consistently end without a named next step. The relationship is being maintained but not advanced.
- Repair attempts to ruptures the IS did not cause. They may be over-functioning for someone else's behavior.
- Talk share holding steady when the meeting needed an inflection. Sometimes a deal needs an interruption to the warmth and the IS is constitutionally unlikely to provide it.

## Coaching move

Introduce a name-the-next-step practice at the close of every meeting. The IS must speak one specific commitment with an owner and a date before the meeting ends. Watch the next session for whether they say it out loud rather than sending it in the follow-up email. If they do, the relationship work is being capitalized rather than carried forward indefinitely.
