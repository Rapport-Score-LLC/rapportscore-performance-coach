# DISC Style: Pure I / Influencer

Session-observed communication style guidance. Not personality destiny. People flex across contexts. Verify across multiple interactions.

## Headline

A pure Influencer wins the room before the room knows the agenda. They are the team member most likely to convert a stranger into a willing collaborator inside the first five minutes.

## In conversation

Influencers run high talk share early in the call and a softer profile by the close. Response latency is very short and often overlapping; they jump in on shared laughter, agreement markers, and storytelling. Question architecture skews toward open, affiliative, and rapport-checking forms: "what was that like for you," "how did you land there." Closed and challenge questions are uncommon and feel uncomfortable when used. Interruption profile is dominated by cooperative interruptions: finishing sentences, layering excitement, building on a teammate's thread. Lexical entrainment (LSM) with the counterpart is the highest of any DISC signature; Influencers mirror diction, pace, and register without conscious effort. Repair attempts are quick and frequent because they detect ruptures fast. Topic-shift authorship is high, often toward stories and analogies rather than next-step content.

## Team contribution

Influencers are most valuable in the opening third of a meeting, in any first contact with a new stakeholder, and in moments where the team needs to rebuild a damaged relationship. They generate the trust currency that the rest of the team spends later. Without an Influencer present, teams often close deals technically and lose the relationship that would have produced the next three.

## Allowable weakness

Influencers can drift, follow the energy in the room rather than the agenda on the page, and overcommit on enthusiasm that other team members then have to deliver against. Their comfort with ambiguity is high by design; this is what lets them connect, and also what makes them an unreliable closer.

## Watch for

- An Influencer holding talk share into the closing third of a call. The deal is usually being relationship-bought, not commitment-bought.
- Cooperative interruption rate dropping. Usually means the counterpart has lost engagement, not that the Influencer has improved restraint.
- Repair attempts on ruptures that never happened. Sometimes Influencers smooth over the friction the team actually needed to feel.

## Coaching move

Before any meeting, have the Influencer name the one commitment they will surface from the counterpart by the end of the call. Watch the next session for whether they ask the closing question rather than absorbing the answer into a story. If they do, the warmth has bought a decision and not just a feeling.
