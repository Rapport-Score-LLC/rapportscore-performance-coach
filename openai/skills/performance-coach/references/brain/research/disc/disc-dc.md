# DISC Style: DC / Driver-Conscientious

Session-observed communication style guidance. Not personality destiny. People flex across contexts. Verify across multiple interactions.

## Headline

A DC lands the call and defends it with data in the same breath. They are the team member most likely to close a decision and have the supporting evidence already loaded.

## In conversation

DC speakers carry a Driver's closing profile (high talk share, short latency at the close) layered with a Conscientious's mid-meeting precision. Question architecture has two distinct phases: probing and clarification forms in the middle of the meeting (sourcing numbers, surfacing assumptions), then closed and challenge forms at the close. Open and affiliative questions are scarce. Interruption profile leans competitive, often to insert a clarification mid-statement rather than to push for the close. Topic-shift authorship is low to moderate; DC speakers go deeper into a topic before they advance it, where a pure D would have advanced two topics in the same time. Lexical entrainment (LSM) is the lowest of any blended DISC; DC speakers use precise diction and expect counterparts to meet them there. Repair attempts are infrequent and tend to be corrective ("to be precise, what I said was") rather than warm.

## Team contribution

DC speakers are the team's defense against the confident-but-wrong close. They are most valuable in decisions where the financial, technical, or reputational cost of a wrong answer is high, and in any conversation with a counterpart who is themselves data-driven. Without a DC, teams with strong D-energy often close on the directionally right answer and discover the exact number was wrong.

## Allowable weakness

DC speakers can come across as cold, transactional, and unwilling to soften a critique for the sake of the relationship. The C-secondary sharpens the directness of the D rather than softening it. They can also stall a decision past its useful window in pursuit of a precision the situation did not require.

## Watch for

- A DC whose probing question rate spikes late in the meeting. Often a tell that they are not yet ready to close and the team is moving anyway.
- Repair attempts after the DC has been factually correct and interpersonally costly. Sometimes the team is doing the repair the DC should have.
- Lexical entrainment (LSM) staying flat across the call. The DC is not adjusting to a counterpart who needs them to.

## Coaching move

Introduce a translate-the-data practice. Before sharing a number that contradicts the room, the DC names what the number means for the person hearing it. Watch the next session for whether the counterpart engages with the data rather than defending against it. If they do, the precision has been received as help rather than as a verdict.
