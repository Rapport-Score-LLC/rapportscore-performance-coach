# DISC Style: IC / Influencer-Conscientious

Session-observed communication style guidance. Not personality destiny. People flex across contexts. Verify across multiple interactions.

## Headline

An IC wins the room and earns the room's respect for the substance behind the warmth. They are the team member most likely to be liked and also believed in the same meeting.

## In conversation

IC speakers carry an Influencer's opening profile but with materially more probing and clarification questions woven through. Talk share is high early and tapers, similar to a pure I, but the words-per-turn signature is denser; the C-secondary fills the IC's contributions with structure where a pure I would have used storytelling. Question architecture is a layered hybrid: open and affiliative forms to build the room, then probing forms ("how did you arrive at that," "what is the source for that") to test the substance. Closed and challenge forms are uncommon. Interruption profile is cooperative-dominant, but the cooperative interruptions are often clarifying ("just to make sure I have that right") rather than confirming. Lexical entrainment (LSM) is high but the diction stays precise; the IC adapts pace and register without loosening accuracy. Repair attempts are frequent and intellectually framed.

## Team contribution

IC speakers are the team's credibility-led relationship builders. They are most valuable in conversations with technical counterparts, expert buyers, and any room where the IC needs to be the warm voice and the substantively correct voice at the same time. Without an IC, teams with strong I-energy can build relationships that the counterpart later doubts intellectually; teams with strong C-energy can be correct and untrusted.

## Allowable weakness

IC speakers can over-research, second-guess their own positioning, and stall a relationship by introducing too much precision into a moment that needed warmth alone. The C-secondary can also undermine the I's natural confidence at the worst possible moment, surfacing a caveat exactly when the counterpart needed conviction.

## Watch for

- An IC whose probing question rate climbs after a relationship moment. Sometimes a tell that they are retreating from the warmth into the safer territory of substance.
- Words per turn rising late in the meeting. The C-secondary may be over-explaining at the close.
- Repair attempts in service of accuracy rather than relationship. ICs sometimes correct counterparts at moments when correction was the wrong tool.

## Coaching move

Introduce a name-the-confidence practice. Before sharing a caveat or qualification, the IC explicitly states what they are confident about first. Watch the next session for whether the counterpart leans in to the qualification rather than away from the IC. If they do, the precision is being heard as honesty rather than as hedging.
