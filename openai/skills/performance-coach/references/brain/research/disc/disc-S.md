# DISC Style: Pure S / Steady

Session-observed communication style guidance. Not personality destiny. People flex across contexts. Verify across multiple interactions.

## Headline

A pure Steady holds the team together during change, conflict, and overload. They are the team member most likely to be the one a teammate quietly checks in with after a hard meeting.

## In conversation

Steady speakers run at or slightly below the team mean on talk share, with a flat profile across the meeting (no spikes at open or close). Response latency is the longest of any DISC signature; they pause before they speak and the pause is deliberate, not unsure. Question architecture skews toward open and confirming forms: "can you say more about that," "did I hear you right that." Challenge and closing questions are rare. Interruption profile is overwhelmingly cooperative when interruptions occur at all, and the overall interruption rate is low; Steady speakers wait. Lexical entrainment (LSM) is high but slower to develop than an Influencer's; once it locks in, it holds across the rest of the conversation. Repair attempts are deliberate, well-placed, and often the most effective in the room. Topic-shift authorship is low; Steady speakers stay with a topic until the team has finished it.

## Team contribution

Steady speakers are most valuable during change windows, conflict, and any conversation where a teammate is processing something difficult. They are the trust anchor of the team. Without a Steady present, teams often handle rupture by avoiding it, and the unfinished emotional content shows up in the next meeting as friction the agenda cannot explain.

## Allowable weakness

Steady speakers can be slow to commit to change, resistant when a decision moves faster than they can process it, and prone to absorb conflict rather than surface it. The same patience that holds the room can stall a conversation that needed to move.

## Watch for

- A Steady going quiet during a high-pressure decision. Often they have a concern they have not yet found a safe way to voice.
- Response latency stretching beyond the personal baseline. Usually a signal that the pace of the meeting has outrun their processing window.
- A Steady absorbing repair work that should have been done by the person who caused the rupture.

## Coaching move

Schedule a five minute pre-read window for the Steady before any meeting that will change a commitment. Watch the next session for whether their first contribution comes inside the first twenty minutes. If it does, the pre-read has bought them the processing time they need to be present rather than absent in the room.
