# DISC Style: Pure C / Conscientious

Session-observed communication style guidance. Not personality destiny. People flex across contexts. Verify across multiple interactions.

## Headline

A pure Conscientious protects the team from the decision that looked right and was not. They are the team member most likely to find the assumption no one else stress-tested.

## In conversation

Conscientious speakers run below the team mean on talk share but above the mean on words per turn when they do speak; their contributions are dense and structured. Response latency is long and intentional; they answer the question that was asked, not the one that came first to mind. Question architecture is dominated by probing and clarification forms: "what is the source for that number," "how was this defined," "what assumption is doing the work here." Open and affiliative questions are rare. Interruption profile is low overall, and the few interruptions are almost always cooperative and corrective ("just to clarify the previous point"). Lexical entrainment (LSM) is low; Conscientious speakers use precise diction and do not loosen it to match the room. Repair attempts are uncommon because they tend to read disagreement as data, not rupture. Topic-shift authorship is low; they go deeper, not sideways.

## Team contribution

Conscientious speakers are most valuable in the middle of a meeting, when the team has surfaced options and now needs the options pressure-tested before commitment. They are the team's defense against the confident-but-wrong answer. Without a Conscientious present, teams often commit to plans that survive the first slide and fail the first quarter.

## Allowable weakness

Conscientious speakers can be slow to commit, critical in tone without intending to be, and resistant to "good enough" when the team has already paid the cost of further analysis. The same precision that catches the bad option can stall the team past the window in which any option would have worked.

## Watch for

- A Conscientious whose questions arrive after the decision has already been made. Often a signal that the team has skipped the pressure-test window they were the one to provide.
- Probing question rate climbing past a personal baseline mid-meeting. Usually means they have detected a flaw the team has not yet named.
- A Conscientious silent in a discovery call. Their highest-leverage behavior is unused.

## Coaching move

Name an explicit pressure-test window on every agenda where the Conscientious is present, and call on them by name at the start of it. Watch the next session for whether the team treats their challenge as data rather than as friction. If they do, the team is using the Conscientious at their highest leverage instead of routing around them.
