# Responsiveness at Blue Depth

Belt bars are whole-rapport only ([[belt-system]]). This note is coaching language for depth of the **responsiveness** dimension near the blue performance band. Do not award a per-dimension belt.

## Headline

Blue Belt Responsiveness means the rep has crossed from reliable coverage into real-time awareness: energy shifts register within the turn, and the behavioral response has begun to follow, even if execution is not yet consistent across the session.

## In conversation

The clearest marker is latency modulation at high-stakes moments. When the other person's energy shifts (shorter sentence length, slower speech rate, uncharacteristic silence), response latency extends before the rep's next turn. The extension is modest, typically under two seconds above baseline, and registers in session data as a pattern.

Talk share begins to shift in real time. When the other person's replies become clipped, the rep pulls back; when their energy rises, the rep follows. This dynamic adjustment is the core Blue Belt signal. It is not yet fluid: the rep reverts to their stable default during agenda transitions in the middle third of the call.

Implication questions now appear in the second half of the call: "what does this mean for the rollout," "how does your team feel about the current pace." They tend to follow a beat after an energy shift, but their presence alters the shape of the conversation.

Speech rate begins to track the other person's during emotionally weighted segments, narrowing the gap between the rep's natural tempo and the other person's in-the-moment rhythm.

## Team contribution

A Blue Belt Responsiveness member brings a different quality of presence to calls with emotional weight. In conversations where trust is the rate-limiter, their session patterns correlate with longer engagement duration and higher disclosure rates from the other side.

## Allowable weakness

The adjustment is real but not yet automatic. Blue Belt shows a within-session consistency gap: strong in the opening and closing, with reversion to default in the middle, especially during agenda transitions. When the rep is managing content complexity, attunement competes with the task layer and loses temporarily.

## Watch for

- Talk share reverts to the stable default during agenda transitions even when the other person's energy has shifted.
- Implication questions cluster in the second half of the call rather than in response to specific energy-shift moments; timing is improving but not yet moment-sensitive.
- Speech rate accommodation drops during content-heavy segments, creating a tempo mismatch that can read as the rep having moved on while the other person is still processing.

## Coaching move

The Blue-to-Purple transition requires moving from reactive attunement (adjusting after an energy shift) to proactive attunement (adjusting in anticipation of where energy is heading). In the next three to five roleplay sessions, the rep sets one intention: to name an energy observation mid-conversation, not in the debrief. After each session, the rep reviews call data and asks whether their adjustment came before or after the other person's explicit signal. When adjustments consistently precede explicit signals, the Purple gate is ready to evaluate.
