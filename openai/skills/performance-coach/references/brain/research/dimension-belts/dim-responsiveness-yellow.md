# Responsiveness at Yellow Depth

Belt bars are whole-rapport only ([[belt-system]]). This note is coaching language for depth of the **responsiveness** dimension near the yellow performance band. Do not award a per-dimension belt.

## Headline

Yellow Belt Responsiveness means explicit content lands, questions get answered, nothing major falls through the cracks, but the layer beneath the words is not yet consistently in reach.

## In conversation

Three stable signals define this band. Response latency is consistent session over session, but latency modulation is absent: when emotional weight surfaces, latency holds flat rather than extending to create processing space.

Talk share is stable. The rep does not monopolize, but when the conversation calls for more space or more presence, talk share does not shift. The rep holds their default allocation because nothing in the explicit content has signaled a change.

Question architecture is predominantly content-level: "what happened," "what have you tried," "who is involved." Implication questions are absent precisely when they matter most: after speech rate drops, when sentence length shortens, when the acoustic weight of the moment rises. The sub-surface energy shift has not broken through as a reliable signal.

Backchannels are appropriately timed. The gap is not in listening mechanics; it is in the behavioral output that follows noticing.

## Team contribution

A Yellow Belt Responsiveness member is a reliable partner in conversations that stay at the content level. They do not let things drop, follow up clearly, and the other person leaves with a sense that the exchange accomplished what it set out to accomplish.

## Allowable weakness

This band does not reliably detect when the conversation's emotional register has shifted beneath the surface. A customer who says "yes that makes sense" while their speech rate has slowed and sentence length has shortened is sending a signal that Yellow Belt Responsiveness misses. The rep may exit the call believing it went well because the explicit content was handled, while the other person's unspoken reservation was never surfaced.

## Watch for

- Response latency stays flat across a session where the other person's energy has shifted, including after an emotional disclosure or unexpected objection.
- Talk share holds stable through a stretch where the other person gives brief or clipped replies, signaling they may need more space rather than more content.
- Questions remain content-level through the back half of a call even as topic weight increases: the rep stays in "what" and "how" without shifting toward "what does this mean."

## Coaching move

The Yellow-to-Blue transition turns on one skill: detecting energy shifts within a turn and responding behaviorally, not just cognitively. After each session, the rep identifies one moment where the other person's energy shifted and describes what signal they registered and what they did next. After five calls with this review practice, the rep enters the next roleplay looking for one moment per call to extend latency or shift to an implication question. That behavior change, documented in a completed roleplay at Yellow Belt level, is the evidence the gate is ready to open.
