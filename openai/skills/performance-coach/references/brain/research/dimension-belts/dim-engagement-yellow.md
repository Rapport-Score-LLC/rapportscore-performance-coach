# Engagement at Yellow Depth

Belt bars are whole-rapport only ([[belt-system]]). This note is coaching language for depth of the **engagement** dimension near the yellow performance band. Do not award a per-dimension belt.

## Headline

Yellow Belt Engagement means attention is present and real, but its quality tracks the person's own interest level rather than the other person's need for it.

## In conversation

At Yellow Belt, engagement arrives reliably in novel or high-stakes parts of a conversation and pulls back in familiar or procedural territory. Question quality follows the same curve: specific and probing in high-interest segments, generic or confirmatory in routine ones. Reflection accuracy degrades in lower-interest stretches, producing reflections that capture the surface of what was said rather than the substance.

Response latency consistency is the earliest measurable indicator of this pattern: latency compresses slightly in low-interest segments, signaling the person is responding to their own read rather than waiting for the other person to fully land. The other person's talk share stays roughly flat across the session rather than rising, the key measurement signal that engagement is being received but not yet creating conditions for more. Talk share trajectory distinguishes this level from Blue: the other person brings the same amount in the second half as in the first.

## Team contribution

A Yellow Belt Engagement member is a reliable presence in conversations that matter. They bring full attention to high-stakes moments and topics genuinely new to them, and the authenticity shows. Teams benefit most when working on problems at the edge of what the person knows.

## Allowable weakness

The cost is selectivity. Sophisticated partners notice the difference between how present the person is when the topic is interesting and how present they are when it is not. The engagement is real but conditional on the person's own interest rather than what the moment requires.

## Watch for

- Talk share compression in the middle third signals attention drift rather than deliberate space-giving.
- Question quality dropping to confirmation-seeking in familiar segments is the measurable form of partial engagement.
- The other person's talk share holding flat or declining is the clearest signal engagement is not yet landing as presence.

## Coaching move

The Yellow-to-Blue transition requires engagement quality that holds across the full arc, not just the interesting parts. In roleplay sessions at this belt level, the person selects one segment they find least interesting and commits to holding full engagement through it. They track their own question quality in that segment: was it a question that could only have been asked because they were actually listening, or a confirmatory question anyone would have asked? Three consecutive sessions where question quality in the low-interest segment matches the high-interest segment is the behavioral evidence the Blue Belt threshold is within reach. Roleplay completion and consistency across three to five qualifying calls both need to clear before the Blue Belt gate opens.
