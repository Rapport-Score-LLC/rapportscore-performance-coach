# Active Listening at Purple Depth

Belt bars are whole-rapport only ([[belt-system]]). This note is coaching language for depth of the **active listening** dimension near the purple performance band. Do not award a per-dimension belt.

## Headline

Purple Belt Active Listening means the full PACER sequence now runs naturally across content and emotional registers, and the listener has begun hearing what was meant, not only what was said.

## In conversation

At Purple, the PACER sequence is no longer a deliberate technique. Pause duration holds at two seconds or above across the full arc of the call, including the highest-stakes turns near close. Confirmation appears after both factual and emotional content without a visible decision point. Questions now reach the implication level (for example, "What does this mean for how you're thinking about the decision?"), layering on top of exploration questions rather than replacing them. Reflection accuracy is high: the playback matches what was meant, and occasionally surfaces something the other person had not yet articulated. Silence is a deliberate tool: the listener holds quiet after a reflection, allowing the other person to respond to being heard. Talk share for the listener at Purple typically sits in the 35 to 45 percent range because implication questions and held silence create sustained depth. When stated position and emotional tone diverge, the Purple Belt listener names the gap softly.

## Team contribution

The Purple Belt Active Listener changes the quality of information the team works with. Deal notes from their conversations contain context the customer's own team may not have surfaced. People feel heard by them, and that changes how openly they communicate going forward.

## Allowable weakness

The Purple Belt listener can over-stay on a thread. Session data shows talk-share ratios that are high overall but occasionally spike within a single thread, signaling depth was extended past the natural close.

## Watch for

- Implication questions appear in every session but do not vary by context. Applied uniformly, they feel like a pattern rather than curiosity.
- Reflections consistently match meaning but rarely surface something the other person had not yet articulated. The next register is hearing below the stated meaning.
- Other person's talk share averaging above 65 percent paired with a low question count. The listener is holding back from the one question that opens the next layer.

## Coaching move

The Purple-to-Brown transition targets multi-level listening: hearing the words, the emotional state, what is not being said, and the pattern connecting all three. In roleplay, the listener receives a scenario where those signals do not align: the stated concern is one thing, the tone another, the conversational pattern a third. The task is to name all three in a single reflection, then hold silence. The debrief evaluates whether the reflection was accurate across all three levels and whether the response indicated depth. A qualifying session shows at least one multi-level reflection per call and a measurable disclosure increase following it. Consistency across three to five qualifying sessions gates the transition to Brown.
