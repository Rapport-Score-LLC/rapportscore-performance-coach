# Engagement at Blue Depth

Belt bars are whole-rapport only ([[belt-system]]). This note is coaching language for depth of the **engagement** dimension near the blue performance band. Do not award a per-dimension belt.

## Headline

Blue Belt Engagement means the person has broken the link between personal interest and attention quality, holding full engagement through the routine segments that test whether presence is a choice or a reaction.

## In conversation

The defining shift at Blue Belt is that question quality no longer declines in familiar or procedural segments. Across both stimulating and routine stretches, the person generates questions tied to something specific the other person said rather than where the conversation is supposed to go next. Reflection accuracy holds across the full arc, capturing substance rather than surface even in low-interest segments.

Response latency consistency is the key measurement signal: latency stays close to the within-session baseline throughout, neither compressing in low-interest segments nor extending under complexity. The other person's talk share rises measurably across the arc, which is the trajectory that separates Blue from Yellow. The other person brings more in the second half because consistent engagement is creating room for it.

## Team contribution

A Blue Belt Engagement member raises the quality of conversations they are part of, including the parts no one finds interesting. Teams benefit most in sustained interactions where attention drift is the norm. Consistency through routine segments creates conditions where the other person does not have to calibrate their counterpart's attention before deciding how much to share.

## Allowable weakness

Blue Belt Engagement receives the other person's prepared thinking well but does not yet reliably draw out unprepared thinking. The other person's talk share rises, but what they say is largely what they came in prepared to say. The other person feels heard but not necessarily expanded.

## Watch for

- Question quality remaining high but generic: well-crafted questions that could fit any conversation on this topic rather than only this exchange.
- The other person's talk share plateauing in the back half signals engagement is holding without accelerating.
- Reflection accuracy staying high but predictable: capturing what was said without surfacing what was implied.

## Coaching move

The Blue-to-Purple transition is about engagement that elevates rather than receives. In roleplay sessions at this belt level, the person tracks whether the other person's sentence length and idea complexity is higher in the final third than in the opening third. If it is not, the person is receiving well but not drawing out. The training move is to introduce one question per segment that invites the other person beyond their prepared thinking: not "tell me more" but a question that opens a gap between what they said and where it leads. The roleplay debrief checks whether the other person's final-third responses contain material absent from their opening statements. That evidence, sustained across three to five qualifying calls alongside the score threshold and roleplay completion, is what the Purple Belt gate requires.
