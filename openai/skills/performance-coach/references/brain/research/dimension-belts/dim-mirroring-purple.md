# Mirroring at Purple Depth

Belt bars are whole-rapport only ([[belt-system]]). This note is coaching language for depth of the **mirroring** dimension near the purple performance band. Do not award a per-dimension belt.

## Headline

Purple Belt Mirroring means the communicator operates on two channels at once: language and time are both converging with the other person, and breaks are caught and closed before they register as distance.

## In conversation

Purple Belt is distinguished from Blue by the addition of a second convergence channel. Where Blue Belt stabilizes LSM and speech rate convergence, Purple Belt communicators also show convergence in temporal patterns: pause length and response latency track toward the other person's natural rhythm rather than holding at the communicator's own baseline. Break detection and recovery timing become conscious rather than retrospective, with adjustments landing within one or two turns.

LSM scores are consistently high across the full session arc, including objections, competitive comparisons, and commitment discussions where lower belts show dips. The session arc is notably flatter with a higher floor throughout.

Convergence is fast: typically established within the first five minutes. Purple Belt communicators begin to modulate mirroring intensity, holding calibrated partial accommodation when full convergence with a highly informal or highly formal speaker would overshoot.

## Team contribution

A Purple Belt Mirroring member anchors relational quality through the full conversation arc. They are the person the team wants leading difficult conversations, renewal calls, or relationships that have experienced friction. Dual-channel operation makes the other person's experience meaningfully warmer than with a Blue Belt peer, even when the content is hard.

## Allowable weakness

Purple Belt mirroring is still fundamentally a one-to-one capability. Tracking two convergence channels across multiple speakers with divergent styles is harder to sustain. When recovering from a break, the adjustment is effective but can carry a slightly managed quality that a Brown Belt's automatic attunement does not.

## Watch for

- Temporal convergence present in one-on-one calls but absent when a third or fourth participant joins.
- Clean LSM throughout but a slightly stilted turn immediately after break recovery: caught and closed is Purple, but the closing move is still visible.
- Uniformly high LSM even in moments where deliberate divergence would signal appropriate differentiation, indicating mechanical rather than fluid accommodation.

## Coaching move

The Purple-to-Brown transition requires shifting from conscious multi-channel coordination to automatic dual-channel attunement that no longer requires active monitoring.

The assignment for this belt is multi-party practice: in conversations with two or more people with distinct styles, notice which speaker you are settling into rhythm with and whether that is the right relational choice for the conversation's goals. The debrief question after each session is where did shared rhythm come naturally, and where did you have to consciously return to it. The roleplay for the Purple-to-Brown gate uses a two-person counterpart scenario with meaningfully different styles, one fast and informal and one measured and formal. Consistent dual accommodation across three to five consecutive sessions is the gate for Brown.
