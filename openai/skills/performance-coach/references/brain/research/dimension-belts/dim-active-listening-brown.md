# Active Listening at Brown Depth

Belt bars are whole-rapport only ([[belt-system]]). This note is coaching language for depth of the **active listening** dimension near the brown performance band. Do not award a per-dimension belt.

## Headline

Brown Belt Active Listening means the listener operates at four levels simultaneously, and their presence alone changes what the other person is willing to say.

## In conversation

Brown Belt Active Listening is not identifiable by any single metric. It shows up in the relationship between metrics. Pause duration holds, but more notable is that pauses are placed: they arrive at the exact moment the other person is sitting with something they have not yet named. Confirmation and reflection appear less frequently than at Purple. Where a Purple Belt listener reflects two or three times across a thread, the Brown Belt listener reflects once and the other person discloses more. Question architecture is exploration and implication, used sparingly, with almost no content questions in the transcript. The most measurable signal at Brown is the other person's talk share arc: their share of speaking time rises steadily across the call, often reaching 65 to 75 percent by the final third. Topics in the back half consistently include things the other person had not planned to say. Response latency and silence patterns function as precision instruments, not defaults.

## Team contribution

The Brown Belt Active Listener is a team resource whose impact extends beyond any individual call. Customers and teammates who have spoken with them once tend to seek them out again. In deal contexts, they consistently surface the real constraint: the unnamed decision-maker, the timeline that looks firm but is not, the concern the customer's own team is managing around.

## Allowable weakness

Brown Belt Active Listening can surface a gap peers and customers had not previously named. The contrast effect raises implicit expectations for future conversations. The listener holds more information than lower belts do and must be deliberate about how it is used.

## Watch for

- Talk share arc for the other person is flat start to finish. The listener has stopped creating conditions for something unexpected to emerge.
- Question architecture shifting back toward exploration at the expense of implication. Wide rather than precise is a comfort pattern.
- Listener's talk share drops below 30 percent consistently. Passive reception, not active listening.

## Coaching move

Complete at least one roleplay session targeting the four-level listening practice at Brown Belt before the advancement gate evaluates, and confirm the pattern holds across the qualifying call window. Brown Belt stabilization targets listening at the pattern level: hearing what the full conversational arc reveals that no single statement does. After each call, the listener writes two sentences on what the other person's pattern revealed. In the next call, they bring that as a hypothesis, naming it directly or asking one calibrated question to surface it. The gate marker is the other person confirming the hypothesis without being asked, logged across three to five sessions.
