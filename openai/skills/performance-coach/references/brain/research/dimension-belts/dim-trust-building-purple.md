# Trust Building at Purple Depth

Belt bars are whole-rapport only ([[belt-system]]). This note is coaching language for depth of the **trust building** dimension near the purple performance band. Do not award a per-dimension belt.

## Headline

Purple Belt Trust Building means the other person says things in this conversation they have not said elsewhere, and the practitioner made that possible without asking for it.

## In conversation

At Purple Belt, trust is created before it is needed. Opening moves front-load transparency and acknowledgment of what is uncertain. Commitment signals are precise: not "I'll follow up" but "I'll have the summary to you by noon Thursday, and if I hit a problem, I'll tell you by Wednesday." That specificity builds a cumulative record the other person can verify.

The other person's question-asking rate climbs from the first quarter through the last. By the midpoint, questions are substantive: what the practitioner genuinely thinks, what has gone wrong in similar situations. Hedging frequency drops measurably; sentences in the final segment are shorter and more direct.

Response latency after friction moments stays regulated even when the other person's latency compresses or extends under pressure. The practitioner does not mirror the other person's stress response; they anchor it. Acknowledging the friction moment produces a disclosure that would not have happened in a smoother conversation.

## Team contribution

A Purple Belt Trust Building member is the practitioner the team sends into conversations where getting to the real issue matters most. Customers surface information that would not appear until much later in the relationship. Colleagues who are holding back resolve that hold-back.

## Allowable weakness

The psychological safety they create depends on their direct presence. They are effective one-on-one and in small-group settings where they can track language shifts in real time. In large group settings or fast-moving conversations, individual attunement becomes harder to sustain. They are not yet building trust at the level of norms that holds when they are not in the room.

## Watch for

- The other person's question-asking rate climbs but then plateaus or drops in the final segment. The practitioner got the conversation elevated but did not hold it through the close.
- Commitment precision is high on volunteered commitments but drops on those arising from the other person's questions.
- Recovery from friction ends at restoration rather than deepening. The Purple-to-Brown move requires friction to become an asset.

## Coaching move

The Purple-to-Brown transition requires a completed roleplay at Purple Belt level and scores sustained across three to five consecutive qualifying calls. The behavioral target is systemic transparency: naming what is happening in the conversation, not just what is being discussed. The practice is to name one dynamic per significant conversation rather than only a content point, for example: "I notice we have been moving quickly through this and I want to make sure we get to what is actually in the way." In roleplay, practice the conversation that has gone well on the surface but where the real issue has not been reached. The Brown Belt move is to name that sensing directly.
