# Responsiveness at Purple Depth

Belt bars are whole-rapport only ([[belt-system]]). This note is coaching language for depth of the **responsiveness** dimension near the purple performance band. Do not award a per-dimension belt.

## Headline

Purple Belt Responsiveness means real-time attunement is running continuously across the session: the rep tracks energy, adjusts presence, and makes deliberate choices about when to step forward and when to hold back, all without visible effort.

## In conversation

The defining characteristic at Purple Belt is behavioral adjustment not triggered by an explicit signal. The rep reads energy trajectory from speech rate, sentence length, and pause pattern, adjusting while the other person is still forming their next sentence. Session data shows latency modulation that precedes the other person's explicit emotional content.

Response latency is fully dynamic. In fast-moving exchanges, latency compresses. When emotional weight enters, latency extends deliberately, creating two to four seconds of space. Session data shows an asymmetry: latency extension correlates with subsequent disclosure by the other person, not with the rep re-engaging.

Talk share is fluid and non-default: below fifty percent during emotionally weighted segments, above during reconnection or orientation segments. Speech rate converges with the other person's across the session; by the midpoint, the rep's tempo is within ten to fifteen percent of the other person's natural rhythm. Question architecture moves freely between content, implication, and silence, governed by the moment.

## Team contribution

A Purple Belt Responsiveness member changes the quality of conversations that carry real stakes. In renewal discussions and complex discovery calls, their session data correlates with higher rates of unsolicited disclosure and more customer-originated agenda items in follow-up conversations.

## Allowable weakness

Purple Belt Responsiveness is effortful to sustain in high-volume periods. Because attunement is deliberate rather than fully automatic, it draws on a finite attention resource. When session load is high, the attunement layer compresses toward a stable default. Brown Belt is where attunement becomes autonomous enough that it no longer competes for that resource.

## Watch for

- Latency extension and talk-share adjustment appear in the opening and closing thirds but compress through a high-density middle: attunement is present but not yet fully sustained under load.
- Implication questions appear reliably but with a one-turn lag after the energy shift; the rep is tracking accurately but the behavioral translation is still a beat behind.
- Speech rate convergence holds during lower-stakes exchanges and degrades during objection-handling stretches.

## Coaching move

The Purple-to-Brown transition requires shifting attunement from deliberate to embedded. In the next three to five roleplay sessions at Purple Belt level, the rep carries one instruction: do not manage the conversation's energy consciously. Set the intention before the call ("I am going to be with this person, not track this person") and review session data afterward to see whether attunement signals held without deliberate effort. When post-session data shows consistent latency modulation, speech rate convergence, and talk-share adjustment across the full session including highest-load segments, and the rep reports they were not consciously executing those moves, the gate is ready to evaluate.
