# Research Layer (Coach Science)

This folder is the science and style-coaching layer imported from the RapportScore Research Vault v2 (TeamOS coaching library, frameworks, research risks). It deepens Wendy and Ask Coach without replacing product measurement notes.

## How to use

1. For what a product number means: read [[Home]] measurement notes first.
2. For why the science holds and where it breaks: read [[research-risks]] and [[signal-catalog]].
3. For session style adaptation (DISC / TKI / pairs): use style notes as **observed communication behavior**, never identity or personality destiny.
4. For belt-depth coaching language by dimension: use [[dimension-belt-ladders]].
5. Labs, OCEAN trait inference, Belbin team-role products, and acoustic-only stacks: see [[labs-and-boundaries]]. Do not teach excluded registry keys as customer data.
