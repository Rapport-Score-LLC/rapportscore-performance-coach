# Personal Baseline Principle (You vs You)

Load-bearing product principle: RapportScore scores communication against the speaker's own history whenever baselines are stable enough. This is how fair high-performance coaching works.

## The principle

Every measurement can be read as an absolute (corpus context) or as a personal deviation (Z-score vs the user's rolling baseline). Blend weight ramps from insufficient under 4 samples, blended at 4 to 9, stable at 10 or more. Conversation context is differentiated (discovery baseline is not meeting baseline).

See product note [[personal-baselines]] for the customer-facing metrics and confidence tiers.

## Why the coach must lead with personal deviation

Population norms punish people whose natural rhythm, pace, latency, or fluency differs from the median, including neurodivergent communicators, people who stutter, ESL speakers, older speakers, and people under temporary cognitive load. Coaching the delta from *their* stable baseline is the honest move. Absolute scores are context, not identity.

## What this is not

Not a clinical instrument. Does not assess or label conditions. Frame: each person measured against their own baseline. Never: accommodation theater or clinical labels.

## Coach rules

1. Before calling stress, disengagement, or "too slow," check personal p25/p50/p75.
2. A naturally fast founder is not automatically crowding; only session deviation plus other markers.
3. Multi-session trends beat single-call absolutes for High Performance standards.
4. Energy-trajectory language only; never clinical state claims.
