# Pair Dynamic: Driver and Steady

How two session-measured styles tend to interact. Anchor claims to pair LSM, latency asymmetry, interruption direction, and repair rates when data exists.

## Headline

A Driver and a Steady combine engine and stabilizer: the D moves the work forward, the S holds the team together while it moves. When functional, this pair turns velocity into sustainable execution.

## What this pairing looks like

A Driver (D) and a Steady (S) are the most directionally asymmetric pair on the DISC matrix. The D leads with urgency and decisiveness; the S leads with patience and relationship continuity. In practice the pair looks like one person setting the pace and another person making sure no one gets left behind. The asymmetry is functional when the S's concerns reach the D; it becomes a vulnerability when the S absorbs rather than surfaces them.

## Team contribution
This pair is highest-leverage on execution windows where the work is well-defined and the team needs both momentum and a stabilizer to carry the rest of the group through it. They are the pair best suited to a long-running project that needs an engine and an anchor in the same body of work. The D names the next move, the S makes sure the team is still with them.

## Allowable weakness
This pair will struggle with shared decision-making, with conversations that require the S to push back on the D's call, and with any work where the S's concerns need to land before the D commits. The asymmetry makes genuine disagreement hard to surface in real time; the S will absorb rather than push back, and the D will not register that the absorption happened.

## In conversation

Pair-level LSM is asymmetric. The S adapts to the D's diction and pacing; the D rarely adapts back. Pair-level response latency is split: the D answers fast, the S answers deliberately, and the gap is wider than any other pair on the matrix. Interruption directionality is one-directional and almost entirely from D to S, mixed in form but with competitive interruptions rising under pressure. The S rarely interrupts in either direction. Repair-attempt frequency is asymmetric and weighted heavily on the S side; the S does most of the repair work after the D causes rupture the D did not notice. Turn-taking balance tilts toward the D, often substantially. Topic-shift authorship is concentrated on the D side. Backchannel signals from the S are consistently present and warm; the D's backchannel output is minimal, which amplifies the directional asymmetry that already shows in talk share and latency. Pair_safety_score reads high on the D's side of the rs_graph_edges row and lower on the S's side; this asymmetry is the cleanest pair-level safety tell on the matrix. Compatibility_score is strong on execution and weak on shared decision-making.

## Watch for: directional note

This dynamic is meaningfully directional. If the D outranks the S, the lock-in risk is severe: the D mistakes the S's quiet compliance for genuine buy-in, and the team commits to a plan the S privately disagrees with. If the S outranks the D, the dynamic inverts; the S can use silence as a slow brake on the D's velocity, and the D experiences the brake as obstruction. Coach for the directional variant the report shows.

## Watch for

- Talk share for the pair concentrated heavily on the D. Standard for this combination, but a tell when combined with the next item.
- S's response latency stretching across successive sessions. The pace of the D has outrun the S's processing window.
- A decision the D closed and the S then privately raised concerns about with a third party. The buy-in was performative.

## Coaching move
Before any decision involving both members, the D names the call and then asks the S one specific question: "what is the thing about this you would change if you could." Then the D stays silent for at least five seconds. Watch the next session for whether the S surfaces a concern inside that window rather than after the meeting. If they do, the pair has converted compliance into genuine buy-in, and the D is getting a truer read of the team than the silence was offering.
