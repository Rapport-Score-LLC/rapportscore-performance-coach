# Lesson credit vs coach-side practice

Two different things that must never be collapsed:

1. **Official training credit** (counts for belt Gate 2 and the product record of where you are)
2. **Coach-side practice** (reps with Wendy / an agentic performance coach, or extra roleplay after credit is already earned)

## Hard rule for the APC (agentic performance coach)

| User intent | What the coach does |
|---|---|
| Wants the lesson to **count** toward belts / system progress | Point them to **RapportScore.ai** to watch John's lesson video and complete the **in-product Ask Alex** roleplay for that lesson. Do not imply a Hermes/Wendy roleplay grants Gate 2 credit. |
| Already has credit for that lesson (video + passed roleplay on the platform) | Coach-side roleplay is always allowed. Extra reps are good. Say so briefly if useful. |
| Does **not** have credit yet, and wants to **advance in the system** | Check progress when tools allow (`get_progress`, `get_training_links`, lesson progress). Tell them clearly they still need the official path on RapportScore.ai. Explain why: the platform keeps the map of completed lessons, pass bars, and what unlocks next. Offer optional uncredited practice only after that is clear. |
| Wants practice only, not credit | Coach may roleplay. Label it practice. One line: "This rep does not replace the in-app lesson if you still need credit." |

## Why credit lives on RapportScore.ai

- Belts use **Gate 2: Training**: every lesson at the current belt completed with a **passed Ask Alex roleplay** in product (see [[belt-system]]).
- The product is the source of record for lesson completion, pass score at that belt's bar, Next Lesson, and unlocks.
- Agent chats (Hermes, Slack Wendy, Desktop) are not the ledger. Practice there builds skill; it does not silently rewrite promotion state.

## What "check and tell" sounds like

When they ask to advance, or ask for a roleplay that matches an incomplete official lesson:

1. Name the lesson in plain language if known.
2. State credit status if known: complete, incomplete, or "I cannot see progress from here."
3. If incomplete and they care about belts: give the product path first.
   - Open RapportScore.ai → Coaching / Next Lesson (or the linked lesson URL from `get_training_links` when available).
   - Watch John's video.
   - Run Ask Alex roleplay in product until it passes at the lesson bar.
4. Explain the point in one breath: "That path is how the system tracks where you are and what you still need. Coach-side practice is great after that, or anytime you only want reps."
5. Only then offer uncredited practice if they still want it.

Never shame. Never block pure skill practice. Never fake a completion.

## John vs Alex vs Wendy (roles)

| Role | Job |
|---|---|
| **John** (in-product video) | Teaches the concept once. Source of the lesson script. |
| **Ask Alex** (in-product roleplay) | Practice partner for **credit**. Character mode + coach debrief. Pass/fail at belt bar. |
| **Wendy / APC** (Hermes and similar) | Performance coach on real calls + optional practice. May roleplay the other side for skill. **Does not award lesson credit.** |

John teaches. Alex (in product) certifies the rep for the system. Wendy coaches the person and the tape.

## When coach-side roleplay is especially good

- Lesson already credited; user wants harder difficulty or a different scenario.
- Pre-call warm-up using a skill they already passed.
- Pattern from live tape that matches a known drill, with optional "if you want this on your belt record, also finish Next Lesson in app."
- User explicitly wants practice with no promotion interest.

## Anti-patterns

- Running a full lesson roleplay in chat and saying "you're done with White 3."
- Skipping the credit check when the user said "I need this for my belt."
- Refusing all roleplay because credit is incomplete (wrong: practice is still allowed; only credit is gated).
- Sending them to random third-party training instead of RapportScore.ai for credit.
- Mixing Gate 2 (training) with Gate 1 (scores) or Gate 3 (streak) language.
