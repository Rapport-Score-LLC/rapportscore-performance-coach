# Clean Commitments (and the hedges that undo them)

The single highest-leverage language habit the coach trains. A clean commitment has three parts, spoken out loud: **owner + action + date**. "I'll send the revised SOW by Thursday noon." A hedged commitment is a promise pre-loaded with its own excuse: "I'll try," "should be able to," "hopefully by Friday," "let me see what I can do."

## The grammar

**Clean forms (all reinforced):**
- Owner + action + date: "I'll send it by Friday noon."
- The clean conditional: an unknown named plus a resolution date committed: "If legal signs off Tuesday, I'll ship Wednesday." "I don't know yet; I'll confirm a date by Friday."
- The honest no: "I can't take that by Friday. I can do Tuesday. Does that work?"
- Negotiated scope: "I can do A by Friday; B needs next week."

**Hedged forms (flagged at commitment points):** "I'll try," "I can try," "should be able to," "hopefully," "probably by," "I think I can," "we'll aim for," "if all goes well," "might be able to."

## The falsifiers (what never counts as a hedge)

- **Epistemic hedges are honesty.** Hedging an estimate ("that's probably a caching issue") is calibrated speech, not the pattern. The test: are you the owner of the hedged verb, and is it a future action? Only then can it count.
- **Reported speech never counts.** "The client said they'll try" is not you hedging.
- **Quoted and roleplay speech is exempt.**
- The coach never trains you to promise what you cannot know: the clean conditional is the honest alternative to false certainty.

## Why it matters

A hedge is heard: commitment language sits inside [[trust-building]] and conviction, and prospects register the escape hatch even when they do not name it. Hedges also compound: people who hedge to others usually hedge to themselves. The healthy trajectory across a call is "we" language early (partnership) shifting to "I" language late (ownership of next steps); five closing next steps that are all "we should" is the ownerless anti-pattern.

## Drills

- One clean commitment per call, spoken as owner + action + date, verified in the next session's data. See [[drills-library]].
- Owner-and-date close: end every call assigning each next step an owner and a date, out loud.
- The renegotiation rep (when overloaded): decline one request cleanly on a recorded call and offer the date you can own.

## FAQ

**Is it ever right to not commit?**
Yes, and the clean version of not committing is a commitment too: name the unknown and commit to when you will know. What the coach flags is the middle ground, promises with escape hatches.

**The coach read my hedge back to me. Why?**
Because a hedged reply to a coaching commitment is the pattern showing up live. It invites the clean version once; what you do with that is yours.
