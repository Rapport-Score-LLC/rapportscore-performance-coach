# Training curriculum map (coach index)

Product training library for communication skill (John video + Ask Alex roleplay). Roughly fifty core `belt_lessons` rows in product docs (belt-specific plus general knowledge), with a larger lesson surface sometimes cited as 120+ when other content is included. **Live product Next Lesson and training links win** if this index drifts.

**Credit rule:** completing a row below for belt progress means video + passed Ask Alex **on RapportScore.ai**. APC roleplay does not grant credit. See [[lesson-credit-and-practice]].

## How the APC uses this map

1. Match the user's live-tape pattern to one core skill below.
2. If they want advancement or credit: send them to RapportScore.ai for that lesson (or Next Lesson if unsure).
3. If they already have credit: optional harder APC roleplay on the same skill is fine.
4. Prefer **one** lesson focus per coaching thread.

## Science hygiene (when pointing at lessons)

Do not restate discredited framing as product truth. Prefer measurement-linked language:

- No NLP-as-science claims; teach acknowledge-before-redirect and reflective listening under real names.
- No "human pheromones" or contested mirror-neuron pitches on phone/video calls.
- "90 seconds" is a training window / thin-slice idea, not a peer-reviewed magic cutoff (see product science sweep notes).
- Psychological safety is a team construct in product measurement; do not redefine it as one person's vibe broadcast.
