# Trust Building

One of the seven Rapport Dimensions. Trust Building measures your effectiveness at building trust through consistency, reliability, and creating safety over the course of the conversation.

## Why it matters

People commit when they feel safe. Candor and consistency compound across every exchange.

## The observable behavior

Volunteer a limitation honestly before they have to discover it.

## Transfer plan (from your coaching path)

- **Do**: name one trade-off or limitation yourself, plainly.
- **When**: when the conversation turns to fit, risk, or comparisons.
- **Proof**: your Trust Building score rises on your next analyzed call.

## How to read your score

Scored 0-100 on the Trust and Rapport radar; gates belts at the shared bar (see [[belt-system]]). Trust Building is where commitment language lives: how you promise, how you hold positions, and how consistently your words line up across the call.

## Related measurements

- [[trust-indicators]]: linguistic trust signals (commitment language, appropriate openness, consistency of promises).
- [[clean-commitments]]: the owner-action-date grammar; hedged commitments are the fastest measurable drag on trust language.
- [[psychological-safety]]: your running acknowledgment-and-repair profile across sessions.
- [[recovery-episodes]]: whether friction gets repaired, a strong trust behavior.

## Drills

- One clean commitment per call, spoken as owner plus action plus date, verified in the next session's data. See [[drills-library]] and [[clean-commitments]].
- Volunteer the trade-off: name one honest limitation before being asked, once per relevant call.
- Truthful risk-reversal: where it is true, make it safe to say yes ("if it's not a fit, we part friends"). It must be real; invented safety is deception and the coach will not help with that (see [[coaching-promises]]).

## FAQ

**Is trust really measurable?**
The system measures trust-relevant language and behavior: commitment phrasing, consistency, repair, acknowledgment. It never claims to read minds; it counts what was said and when.

**My Trust Building lags my other dimensions. Where do I start?**
Commitment points. Count your hedges in the closing minutes ("hopefully", "I'll try", "should be able to") and replace one per call with the clean form. See [[clean-commitments]].
