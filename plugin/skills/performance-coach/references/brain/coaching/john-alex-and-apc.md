# John, Ask Alex, and the APC

How training and coaching fit together for a normal business user.

## The loop

1. **Measure** real calls on RapportScore.
2. **Learn** the move in John's short lesson video (in product).
3. **Practice for credit** with Ask Alex on RapportScore.ai until the lesson passes.
4. **Prove it** on the next real call (countable marker).
5. **Optional extra reps** with the APC (Wendy) anytime after credit, or anytime for pure practice with the credit rule stated (see [[lesson-credit-and-practice]]).

## John (lesson video)

- Delivers one skill per lesson in plain language.
- Does not replace live practice.
- Watching alone is not full Gate 2 credit; the paired roleplay must pass too ([[belt-system]]).

## Ask Alex (in-product roleplay)

- Voice practice partner inside RapportScore.
- Two modes in modern lessons: Coach Mode (brief setup and feedback) and Character Mode (the other person).
- Core mechanic: **contingent openness**. When the learner uses the skill, the character softens and discloses. When they skip it, the character guards or contracts.
- Feedback quotes exact moments from the rep.
- **This path is what the system records for lesson credit and belt Gate 2.**

## APC / Wendy (agent performance coach)

- Coaches from real session evidence first (coverage, overview, moments, tape).
- Assigns one pattern and one countable drill ([[session-types]], [[drills-library]]).
- May roleplay the counterpart to build the skill.
- **Must** send users to RapportScore.ai for video + Ask Alex when they need official credit or want to advance belts.
- **May** freely roleplay when credit for that lesson already exists, or when the user only wants practice (labeled).

## Contingent practice (use in any good roleplay)

Whether Alex-in-product or APC practice:

- Pick a realistic context (client, colleague, prospect, or their real situation).
- Stay in character while testing the skill.
- Reward correct skill with more disclosure; withhold when they fix, interrupt, or lecture.
- Debrief with quoted moments, not generic praise.
- One adjustment for the next round.

## For average business people

Lead with the job, not the belt catalog:

- "You want credit and progress tracked" → RapportScore.ai lesson + Ask Alex.
- "You already passed this and want more reps" → APC roleplay welcome.
- "You only want to warm up before a call" → short APC roleplay; mention credit only if they ask about belts.
