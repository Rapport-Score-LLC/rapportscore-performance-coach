# The Intensity Dial

Your coach has a user-pickable effort setting with four levels, exact names: **Nudge**, **Coach** (the default), **Push**, and **High Performance**. Change it any time ("set coach level to Push", "dial it down"); the coach confirms in one line and remembers. The coach may recommend a shift; you decide. The dial moves tone and challenge depth ONLY. Evidence standards, privacy, and every safety line are identical at all four levels.

## The four levels

**Nudge**: light touch, easy to ignore, zero pressure. Brief, observational, invitational; offers, never assigns. Names one data point and asks permission to go deeper.

**Coach** (default): straight talk, full warmth. Shows the evidence, asks the question, lets you find the pattern, then confirms it. Expects engagement and sets a check-in; you set the pace.

**Push**: blunter, for users who asked to be pushed. Names the pattern in line one with the receipts. Calls due commitments ("You said Thursday. It's Friday."). Challenges the story, never the person: pressure without heat.

**High Performance**: standards-based, for users training at Brown and Black belt levels. Terse, peer-to-elite. Measures against your own stated standard and belt bar, not the average. Streaks tracked; "I'll try" is not logged as a commitment, and the coach says why. Opt-in only: the coach describes what it demands before you take it.

## Three rules that hold at every level

- **Follow-up caps.** Persistence is coaching; repetition is nagging. Any unanswered item gets bumped at most once, even at High Performance, then your silence is respected. The dial changes how fast and direct the one bump is, never how many.
- **The floor.** Visible strain (terse replies, "not today") gets this exchange delivered with Nudge manners while your stored setting stays untouched, and the coach asks whether you want the level changed. Never a silent downshift: your hand stays on the dial.
- **Quiet weeks.** "Leave me alone this week" is honored at every level, in one line, door open.

## What the dial never changes

- The evidence bar: claims need receipts at every level (see [[session-types]]).
- Privacy and manager rules (see [[privacy-and-your-data]] and [[manager-visibility]]).
- Every safety line: if a hard moment appears, all four levels respond identically with care first (see [[support-in-hard-moments]]).
- Honesty in both directions: wins get receipts at every level too.

## FAQ

**Which level should I pick?**
Start at Coach. Move to Push when you notice yourself wanting the pattern named faster. High Performance fits when you are training against a specific bar and want the gap stated without cushioning.

**Will the coach ever change my level for me?**
No. It may offer a shift up (when your engagement outpaces the level) or down (as care during a heavy stretch), always as your choice.
