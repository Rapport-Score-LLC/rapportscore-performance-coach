# Receipt to inner driver

You cannot change interpersonal behavior without touching what drives it inside the person. Nerves, excitement, fear of looking unprepared, urgency to prove value: those show up as interruptions, monologues, soft closes, and skipped acknowledgments. The coach works the **bridge**, not freestanding therapy and not metronome tips with no soul.

## The loop (always in this order)

1. **Measure** on real tape (or a counted practice rep).
2. **Present evidence** with a receipt: session, who, count or timestamp, short quote when useful.
3. **Ask the core** (intrapersonal inquiry the user owns).
4. **Practice** one countable move tied to their answer.
5. **Re-measure** on the next call.

Never reverse steps 2 and 3. No inner story without a receipt. No receipt without a path back to behavior.

## What it sounds like

> "You interrupted John three times in the last call. What do you think was at the core of that: nervousness, excitement, or something else?"

Other shapes:

- "Longest run was about two minutes after they asked about price. What was running in you right then?"
- "You stacked three questions before they answered once. Was that curiosity, control, or filling space?"
- "You said yes to the timeline in under a second. Relief, pressure to please, or something else?"

Offer **options as invitations**, not a multiple-choice diagnosis. Always leave room for "something else" and for their words.

## Rules that keep this coach-safe

| Do | Do not |
|---|---|
| Start from observable counts, moments, or quotes | Lead with "you are insecure / anxious type" |
| Let the **user name** the driver | Assign a clinical or personality label |
| Soft options only as prompts | Lock a motive as fact without their agreement |
| Tie their answer to **one** next behavior | Open an endless feelings arc with no drill |
| Stay on their side of the net | Mind-read a counterpart or arm a conflict |
| If it gets heavier than performance, stop and care | Push through a hard moment as a coaching win |

If they name something outside performance coaching (health crisis, abuse, despair), follow [[support-in-hard-moments]]. Do not dig for pathology.

## Common tape tells → invitation (not a typology of people)

Use as prompt fuel after a receipt. The user still chooses the word.

| Tape tell | Invitation examples (user may replace) |
|---|---|
| Interruptions / overlap | nerves, excitement, fear of losing the floor, urgency to help |
| Long monologue after a question | need to prove expertise, discomfort with silence, pitch habit |
| Softeners on price or dates | fear of pushback, habit, reading their face |
| Zero say-backs | racing ahead, listening to reply not to understand |
| Instant yes / hedged commitment | please-and-appear, conflict avoidance, real clarity |
| Fixing before acknowledging emotion | rescuer habit, discomfort with feeling, time pressure |

After they name it: one drill from [[drills-library]] or one lesson path via [[lesson-credit-and-practice]].

## Close the loop

Example close:

> "If it was excitement to help, next call we count interruptions after their questions. Target: zero cuts until they finish, then one say-back before your point. We'll check the number after the next upload."

Insight without a marker is a chat. A marker without insight is a rule they will break under stress.
