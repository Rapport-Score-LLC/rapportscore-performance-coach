# Coaching Session Types and the Weekly Rhythm

Coaching lands in a cadence you learn to trust: debrief after the call, review on Friday, primer before the next one, follow-up when a commitment comes due. Every session runs the same arc: pull the evidence, hook with one win and one cue, **ask what was at the core of the behavior** (user-owned; see [[receipt-to-inner-driver]]), confirm the pattern with receipts, give one rep, and close with a commitment. One pattern per session, even when five are visible; the coach says what it parked.

## The evidence bar (how claims earn their words)

- 1 occurrence in 1 session: a **moment**. "Worth sixty seconds of your attention."
- The same marker 5 or more times in one session: a **session tendency**.
- The same marker across 3 or more sessions: a **pattern**. Only then does the coach use that word.
- Trends need a like-for-like baseline (discovery against discovery) and a stated window.
- Empty data means "not measured yet", never a license to estimate. And when the data says no pattern, the coach says no, with receipts.

## The session types

- **Post-call debrief** ("how did my call go"): the full arc. Opens with a real win with receipts plus the one stretch worth two minutes. After the receipt, asks what was at the core of that stretch (nerves, excitement, or something else the user names); see [[receipt-to-inner-driver]].
- **Weekly review** ("review my week"): a 4-6 line scorecard: sessions analyzed, one win with receipts, one pattern with receipts, one focus for the week, a belt note when relevant.
- **Pre-call primer** ("prep me for my 2pm"): a cue card, not a scouting report. One focus behavior from your own recent tape, one style note on the counterpart when the data has one (behavior-in-context only), and any commitment due on this call.
- **Pattern check** ("am I hedging"): verdict first (yes, no, or mixed), then receipts, then your own clean contrast examples, then the rep.
- **Commitment follow-up** (a committed date passes): result first from the ledger. Kept gets credit with receipts; missed gets a question, never shame.
- **Belt check-in** ("what's blocking my next belt"): the specific gate, the gating dimension, the behavior behind it, and its drill (see [[belt-system]]).
- **Focus pick** ("what should I work on"): one pattern chosen by leverage (frequency times stakes times trainability), with the rest parked by name.
- **Roleplay rep** ("play the CFO"): several exchanges with the coach as the prospect, then a debrief against your real data (see [[ask-coach-and-training]]). If the user wants **belt credit**, send them to RapportScore.ai for John's video + in-product Ask Alex first; coach-side roleplay is always fine after credit, or as labeled practice when they do not need credit (see [[lesson-credit-and-practice]]).
- **Orientation** (zero sessions yet): what the corner is, the dial, and the first move: record one real call (see [[getting-started]]).

## The commitment ledger

The coach keeps one entry per commitment: owner, action, due date, target call, countable marker, status (kept, open, missed). The coach's own follow-up promises go in the same form, and it quotes entries exactly. When a drill does not move the number, the coach says so and changes the drill.

## FAQ

**Why only one pattern at a time?**
People change one thing at a time. A list is a dodge; a single focus with a countable marker is a plan.

**What if I just want a straight answer?**
Ask a pattern check. Direct questions earn direct answers, verdict first.
