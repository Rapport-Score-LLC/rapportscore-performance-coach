# DISC Style: CD / Conscientious-Driver

Session-observed communication style guidance. Not personality destiny. People flex across contexts. Verify across multiple interactions.

## Headline

A CD pressure-tests the decision and then closes it with the evidence already on the table. They are the team member most likely to be both the most rigorous voice and the most willing to commit.

## In conversation

CD speakers carry a Conscientious's mid-meeting profile (low talk share, long latency, dense turns, high probing question rate) layered with a Driver's late-meeting decisiveness. Response latency is long in the middle of the meeting and short at the close. Question architecture is dominated by probing and clarification forms early, then closed and challenge forms appear at the close: "given what we just established, are we agreed on the next step." Open and affiliative questions are very rare. Interruption profile is mostly cooperative-clarifying in the middle and shifts toward competitive at the close; the D-secondary licenses the CD to override late ambiguity. Lexical entrainment (LSM) is low; the CD uses precise diction throughout and does not loosen it. Repair attempts are infrequent and tend to be corrective rather than warm. Topic-shift authorship is low to moderate, oriented toward depth rather than breadth.

## Team contribution

CD speakers are the team's rigorous closers. They are most valuable in technical decisions, financial commitments, and any conversation with a counterpart who needs the substance pressure-tested before they will commit. The C-primary tests the option; the D-secondary lands the decision. Without a CD, teams often produce well-analyzed work that does not convert into action, or actioned work that did not survive the analysis.

## Allowable weakness

CD speakers can come across as cold, transactional, and unwilling to soften their critique for the sake of the room. The combined C and D signature is the most directness-heavy of any DISC blend; warmth is not a strong native register. They can also escalate sharply if they feel the team is committing to a position the analysis does not support.

## Watch for

- A CD whose late-meeting competitive interruption rate spikes. Often a tell that the team is moving past the evidence and the CD has stopped trying to find a softer way to flag it.
- Probing question rate climbing while the meeting clock is closing. Sometimes a signal that the CD is not yet ready to commit and the team is.
- Lexical entrainment (LSM) staying flat in a relationship moment that required it.

## Coaching move

Introduce a name-the-agreement practice at the close. Before naming the disagreement, the CD names one specific point they agree with from the prior speaker. Watch the next session for whether the counterpart engages with the disagreement rather than defending against it. If they do, the rigor is being received as a contribution rather than as an attack.
