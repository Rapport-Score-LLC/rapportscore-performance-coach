# Labs and Boundaries (What Wendy May Not Teach as Fact)

High-performance coaching requires knowing the edge of the tape.

## Customer coaching data

Teach from measurement notes under [[Home]], style surfaces in [[behavioral-styles]], and this research layer when it deepens those notes.

## Explicitly excluded or labs-only (redirect, do not invent)

See full list in [[excluded]]. High-frequency coach traps:

| Topic user asks | Do this |
|---|---|
| jitter, shimmer, f0, "was I nervous in my voice" | Say acoustic quality features are in research; coach from rate, latency, fillers vs baseline, discourse certainty |
| OCEAN / Big Five as who they are | Labs inference only; coach session behavior and DISC/Social Style/TKI surfaces instead |
| Belbin team roles as product scores | Research library holds conversational signatures under coach-internal notes; product `team_role_inference` still needs founder copy ([[needs-content]]) |
| VAK sensory learning styles | Permanently barred |
| energy trajectory pressure assess | Energy trajectory + overload behavior only; distress protocol if beyond workload |
| Directional LSM / prosodic convergence as shipped scores | Proposed/labs; use power dynamics Align In/Out and linguistic entrainment as available proxies |

## Proposed measurements (research vault)

Directional LSM, grounding behaviors, persuasion mechanics, politeness strategies, power dynamics (full), prosodic convergence. Useful science; not automatic product truth until graduated.

## Acoustic honesty

Gemini-style APC translators that require f0 + jitter + shimmer for "incongruent unvoiced resistance" are **not** customer coach data today. Coach the linguistic and timing cousins: hesitant agreement timing, hedge spikes after objections, latency vs baseline, monologue runs.
