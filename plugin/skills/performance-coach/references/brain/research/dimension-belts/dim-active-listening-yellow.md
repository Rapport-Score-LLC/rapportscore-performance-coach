# Active Listening at Yellow Depth

Belt bars are whole-rapport only ([[belt-system]]). This note is coaching language for depth of the **active listening** dimension near the yellow performance band. Do not award a per-dimension belt.

## Headline

Yellow Belt Active Listening means the foundation is in place and working under calm conditions, but the practice compresses exactly when it matters most.

## In conversation

Yellow Belt Active Listening shows up as an inconsistent pause profile. The two-second pause before responding appears in low-stakes exchanges where content is factual or logistical. Under pressure, or when the topic carries emotional weight, that pause collapses. Response latency during tense or high-stakes turns drops well below the two-second threshold, which is the clearest single signal that PACER is not yet automatic. Confirmation frequency follows a similar pattern: this listener verifies understanding after factual content but skips the confirm step after statements that carry feeling. The thread advances before the emotional content has been acknowledged. Question architecture is predominantly content-level: who, what, when, and what happened next. These questions move the conversation forward without going deeper into the meaning behind what was said. Talk share for this listener typically sits above 60 percent, because the compressed pause and skipped confirmations leave less room for the other person to fill. Topics close on the listener's schedule rather than at the natural end of the other person's thought.

## Team contribution

A Yellow Belt Active Listener gives the team consistent intake on factual and logistical content. They track what was said, remember the details, and confirm those details accurately. In structured discussions where content is primarily informational, they hold their own and contribute meaningfully. The gap shows up when the conversation turns to meaning, concern, or anything that requires the other person to feel heard before they can go further.

## Allowable weakness

The real cost of Yellow Belt Active Listening is that the listener's presence is conditional. When emotional content surfaces, the confirmation step drops and the question moves to the next topic. The other person registers this even without naming it. Over time, they self-edit: they share the facts and hold the concern. In a B2B context, this can look like good call coverage with persistent deal uncertainty, because the concern driving the uncertainty never made it past the surface.

## Watch for

- Pause duration consistently below two seconds in the back half of calls, when complexity and stakes are typically higher.
- No exploration questions in a call where the other person raised a concern. Content questions only means the surface was covered and the meaning was not.
- The other person volunteers new context in the final five minutes of a call that would have changed the conversation if it had surfaced earlier.

## Coaching move

The immediate Yellow-to-Blue transition practice is the deliberate pause drill: before any response in a roleplay session, the listener must hold a visible two-second pause regardless of what was just said. Once the pause holds consistently in roleplay, the next layer is confirmation targeting: after any statement where the other person named a feeling or a concern, the listener must confirm before advancing. The roleplay session report should show pause duration sustained and at least two emotion-targeted confirmations per session before this gate is considered clear. Talk share data showing the listener consistently above 60 percent is also a flag, because it means they are filling the silence the pause should be creating.
