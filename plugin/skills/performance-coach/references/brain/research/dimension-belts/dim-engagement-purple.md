# Engagement at Purple Depth

Belt bars are whole-rapport only ([[belt-system]]). This note is coaching language for depth of the **engagement** dimension near the purple performance band. Do not award a per-dimension belt.

## Headline

Purple Belt Engagement means the person's presence is actively changing what the other person brings to the conversation, not just receiving it well.

## In conversation

At Purple Belt, the clearest measurable signal is not what the person is doing but what the other person does in response. The other person's talk share rises across the arc: they take more space in the second half, and what they say is more developed and more likely to include material they did not arrive with. Sentence length increases across the session, and the other person begins asking their own questions. These signals can only be produced when engagement creates space rather than merely holds it.

Response latency is consistent and used deliberately: statements are allowed to fully land before the person responds, inviting extension rather than closure. Reflection accuracy surfaces implications the other person recognizes as true but had not yet stated. Talk share is calibrated precisely enough to guide without filling the room. Engagement at this level no longer reads as technique.

## Team contribution

A Purple Belt Engagement member changes the quality of thinking that happens in their presence. Conversations produce more complete problem statements and more honest assessments than the same conversation would with a less engaged partner. Team members often report their thinking is clearer after talking with this person, without being able to say why.

## Allowable weakness

The engagement effects require full presence and time to develop. In high-volume environments or conversations that do not allow the second-half dynamics to build, the signature does not fully manifest. The person is effective in depth; not yet consistently effective in breadth.

## Watch for

- The other person's talk share rising but idea complexity not increasing signals space is being created without new thinking being drawn out.
- Technique becoming visible under pressure is a stability signal worth noting.
- The other person's talk share rising then dropping sharply near the close signals opening conditions without a completed arc.

## Coaching move

The Purple-to-Brown transition is about extending the engagement effect to the other person's access to their own best thinking, not just their willingness to talk more. In roleplay sessions at this belt level, the person identifies one moment where the other person said something good, then tracks whether their next question helped the other person go deeper or moved to the next topic. The Brown Belt pattern is staying with the other person's best thinking long enough for them to extend it. The roleplay debrief looks for at least one developed idea arc per session: the other person's initial statement becoming something more complete rather than being acknowledged and filed. That arc, appearing consistently across three to five qualifying calls alongside the score threshold and roleplay completion, opens the Brown Belt gate.
