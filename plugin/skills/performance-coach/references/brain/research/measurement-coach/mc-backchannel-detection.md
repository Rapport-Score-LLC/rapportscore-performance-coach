# Coach Read: Backchannel Detection

Product measurement note: [[backchannel]]

## What this measures

How often this person makes the small listening sounds (mm-hmm, right, I see, oh wow) that tell a partner to keep going.

## What a high score means

A high backchannel rate means this team member is an audible listener. The speaker hears they are being tracked, and tells better stories because of it. When the variety is also wide (a mix of mm-hmm with specific assessments like really or that makes sense), it indicates a listener following meaning, not just monitoring sound. Team contribution: these are the people others want to brief, because briefing them feels productive.

## What a low score means

A low backchannel rate often means this team member is a quiet listener whose feedback runs through eye contact and notes rather than voice. The partner may not feel heard even when they are. The coaching opportunity is to add a handful of well-placed verbal acknowledgments per minute, particularly during a counterpart's longer narratives.

## How the report uses this

Backchannel rate appears in Section 4 (Communication Dynamics) and feeds the Active Listening band in Section 5. Read it with Reflective Listening to separate audible attention from semantic depth.
