# Coach Read: Silence Mechanics

Product measurement note: [[silence-mechanics]]

## What this measures

How this person uses (or rushes to fill) silence: the duration, placement, and function of pauses across the call, classified as productive, emphatic, uncomfortable, or disconnected.

## What a high score means

A high silence-tolerance score reflects a team member who can sit with a pause. They leave space after asking an important question, use brief silence for emphasis, and read the difference between a partner thinking and a partner stuck. Team contribution: they pull deeper answers out of conversations because the partner has room to formulate them. Especially valuable in negotiation and coaching.

## What a low score means

A low score usually shows up as silence-filling pressure: every pause gets closed, often with reassurance or restatement, before the partner has finished thinking. The coaching opportunity is small and specific: after the next important question, count three seconds silently before saying anything else. Watch what the partner does with the space.

## How the report uses this

Silence Mechanics appears in Section 4 (Communication Dynamics) and feeds Clarity and Emotional Regulation in Section 5. The trajectory across the call matters: rising silence tolerance through the conversation is one of the clearest signals of trust developing in real time.
