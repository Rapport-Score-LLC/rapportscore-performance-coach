# Coach Read: Entrainment

Product measurement note: [[entrainment]]

## What this measures

How much this person's language style, vocabulary, syntax, and hedge patterns converge with their partner's over the course of a call.

## What a high score means

A high composite means this team member is naturally tuning to the people they talk with. They pick up the partner's terminology, mirror sentence structures, and the gap between their hedge rates narrows across the call. That convergence is one of the most empirically validated markers of interpersonal coordination. Team contribution: in dyadic work (coaching, discovery, negotiation) this profile builds connection without effort.

## What a low score means

A low score usually means this team member holds their own style regardless of partner. That can be a strength when the role requires authority or a signature voice. It becomes a coaching opportunity when the role requires the counterpart to feel met. The intervention is small: pick up one piece of the partner's vocabulary in your next response, then notice whether the conversation deepens.

## How the report uses this

Entrainment appears in Section 4 (Communication Dynamics) and feeds Mirroring in Section 5. The composite is suppressed for many-to-many call types (team meetings, board meetings) where dyadic alignment math does not apply.
