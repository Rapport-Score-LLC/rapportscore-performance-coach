# Coach Read: Language Style Matching

Product measurement note: [[language-style-matching]]

## What this measures

How closely this person's function-word patterns (pronouns, prepositions, conjunctions, articles) align with their partner's. These small grammatical particles are produced automatically and resist deliberate manipulation, which is why they are one of the most validated markers of cognitive rapport.

## What a high score means

A high LSM score, especially with a rising trajectory across the call, signals genuine cognitive alignment with the partner. They are not performing rapport; they are coordinating beneath the level of conscious choice. Team contribution: relationship work, partnership conversations, and any context where the goal is sustained collaboration.

## What a low score means

A low score means styles are not converging. This can mean the team member is maintaining authority by holding their own register, or that they are not yet tuned to the partner. Read the trend: a flat-and-low score is a tuning problem, while a high-but-falling score is usually a content disagreement showing up in the function words before it surfaces in the dialogue.

## How the report uses this

LSM appears in Section 4 (Communication Dynamics) and is the strongest input into Mirroring in Section 5. The trajectory matters more than the single number: rising convergence is the signal worth flagging next to the score.
