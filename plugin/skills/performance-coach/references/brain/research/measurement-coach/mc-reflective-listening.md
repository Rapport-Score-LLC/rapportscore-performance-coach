# Coach Read: Reflective Listening

Product measurement note: [[complex-reflections]]

## What this measures

How often this person reflects back the content, feeling, or meaning of what the partner just said, separated into simple reflections (rephrase or restate) and complex reflections (add meaning, name the emotion, continue the paragraph).

## What a high score means

A high score (reflection-to-question ratio above 1.0, healthy share of complex over simple reflections) indicates a team member who follows the partner's attention rather than redirecting it. They "continue the paragraph" the speaker was already on. Team contribution: they unlock disclosure. The data is clear that complex reflections generate noticeably longer subsequent speaker turns than questions do.

## What a low score means

A low score usually means the team member is driving with questions: ask, ask, ask. The information gets surfaced but the partner does not feel deeply heard. The coaching opportunity is to swap every third question for a reflection. Watch what happens to the next utterance. The shift in disclosure depth tends to be immediate.

## How the report uses this

Reflective Listening appears in Section 4 (Communication Dynamics) and is one of the core inputs into Empathy in Section 5. Pair it with MITI Classification: reflective listening is the strongest single behavioral driver of the MI-consistency profile.
