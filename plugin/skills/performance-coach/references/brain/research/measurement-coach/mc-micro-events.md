# Coach Read: Micro Events

Product measurement note: [[timing-critical-moments]]

## What this measures

The small, discrete in-call moments (shared laughter, brief agreements, empathy flashes, name use, callbacks, dismissive acknowledgments, abandoned topics) that accumulate into the felt sense of a conversation.

## What a high score means

A high score, with a healthy positive-to-negative ratio and a diverse repertoire, indicates a team member who keeps relational punctuation flowing. They laugh with rather than at, catch callbacks, use names, and signal empathy in brief moves rather than long set pieces. Team contribution: their conversations feel good to be in, which compounds over time into trust capital.

## What a low score means

A low count, or a narrow repertoire dominated by generic agreements, often means this team member is professional but not yet warm. The substance is fine; the relational layer is thin. The coaching opportunity is to expand the positive repertoire by two or three moves: a deliberate callback to something the partner said earlier, a name use mid-call, a specific (rather than generic) agreement.

## How the report uses this

Micro Events appears in Section 4 (Communication Dynamics) and is one of the strongest inputs into Warmth in Section 5. The first-90-second window is reported separately because it is disproportionately predictive of how the rest of the call lands.
