# Purple Belt

Mastering emotional intelligence and active listening. At Purple your session data shows consistent, intentional communication habits. The bar you hold: every dimension at 78 or better.

## How Purple was earned (the three gates)

1. **Score**: all seven Rapport Dimensions averaged at least 78 over recent completed calls.
2. **Training**: every Blue Belt lesson completed with a passed Ask Alex roleplay.
3. **Consistency**: at least 6 completed calls since the Blue promotion, with the last 3 consecutive calls clearing every bar at 78.

## The path to Brown

1. **Score**: all seven dimensions average at least 85.
2. **Training**: every Purple Belt lesson completed with a passed roleplay (Purple lessons pass at the Purple bar of 78).
3. **Consistency**: at least 8 completed calls since your Purple promotion, and your last 4 consecutive calls each clear every dimension at 85.

Promotion is automatic when all three gates pass. See [[belt-system]].

## Coaching focus at Purple

- At this level, wins come from behavior under pressure, not baseline habits: holding position through objections ([[objection-structure]]), regulation during friction ([[emotional-regulation]]), acknowledgment before content ([[emotional-acknowledgment]]).
- The 4-call streak toward Brown rewards deliberate pre-call intentions. One focus behavior per call, checked against the data afterward, is the sustainable way to hold 85.
- Expect the coach to name what you parked: at Purple there is usually more than one visible improvement, and still only one gets coached at a time.

## FAQ

**Why does the streak get longer here?**
Higher belts certify sustained performance. The window grows (4 consecutive qualifying calls for Brown) because holding a level under varied conditions is the skill being certified.

**Does one weak dimension really hold everything?**
Yes, by design. Belts are whole-rapport. The gating dimension is also your most efficient coaching target; ask "what's blocking my next belt".
