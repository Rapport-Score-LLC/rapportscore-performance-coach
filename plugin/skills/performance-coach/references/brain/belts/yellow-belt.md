# Yellow Belt

Basic competency achieved. At Yellow you can identify strong and weak moments in a session and you have started applying coaching feedback. The bar you cleared to get here: every dimension holding 50 or better across a qualifying streak.

## How Yellow was earned (the three gates)

1. **Score**: all seven Rapport Dimensions averaged at least 50 over recent completed calls.
2. **Training**: every White Belt lesson completed with a passed Ask Alex roleplay.
3. **Consistency**: at least 4 completed calls since starting, with the last 2 consecutive calls clearing every bar.

## The path to Blue

1. **Score**: all seven dimensions average at least 65.
2. **Training**: every Yellow Belt lesson completed with a passed roleplay (Yellow lessons pass at the Yellow bar of 50).
3. **Consistency**: at least 6 completed calls since your Yellow promotion, and your last 3 consecutive calls each clear every dimension at 65.

Promotion is automatic when all three gates pass. See [[belt-system]].

## Coaching focus at Yellow

- Move from noticing to repeating: you can spot a good moment; now reproduce it on purpose.
- Your weakest dimension is your gate to Blue. Ask "what's blocking my next belt" to get the exact dimension and number.
- Typical Yellow-stage work: open-question ratio ([[questions-asked]]), response timing ([[response-latency]]), staying out of monologues ([[talk-time-and-turns]]).

## FAQ

**My average is above 65 but I am still Yellow. Why?**
The gate is per-dimension, not the average. One dimension at 62 holds the gate even if the rest are at 80. The streak requirement also means recent consecutive calls all have to qualify.

**Do harder lessons unlock now?**
Yes. Each belt unlocks lesson content not visible at lower levels.
