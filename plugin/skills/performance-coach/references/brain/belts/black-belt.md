# Black Belt

Communication mastery. Black is mentor and teacher level: your patterns are a model for others and your rapport scores are consistently high. The bar you hold: every dimension at 89 or better, sustained.

## How Black was earned (the three gates)

1. **Score**: all seven Rapport Dimensions averaged at least 89 over recent completed calls.
2. **Training**: every Brown Belt lesson completed with a passed Ask Alex roleplay.
3. **Consistency**: at least 10 completed calls since the Brown promotion, with the last 5 consecutive calls clearing every bar at 89.

Promotion, like every other, happened automatically when the gates passed. See [[belt-system]].

## What Black means (and does not mean)

- It certifies measured, sustained behavior across all seven dimensions on real calls. It is the strongest claim the system makes about anyone.
- It does not mean every call scores 89. It means your qualifying window did, and your habits hold the level.
- It is a communication milestone, never a verdict on worth or overall job performance. No belt is.

## Coaching at Black

- The work shifts from reaching a bar to defending a standard: your own baselines become the reference, and drift against them is the signal ([[personal-baselines]]).
- Black-level users often run the coach at High Performance: terse, standards-based, streaks tracked ([[intensity-dial]]).
- Mentor moves count here: reviewing your own strong tape to articulate what you do, so others can learn it, is Black Belt work.

## FAQ

**Is there anything after Black?**
No further belts. The ongoing game is consistency against your own history and helping raise the people around you (see [[manager-visibility]] for how team-level views work).

**Can I lose Black?**
No. Belts never move backward. Scores still fluctuate call to call, and the coach will still name drift when the data shows it.
