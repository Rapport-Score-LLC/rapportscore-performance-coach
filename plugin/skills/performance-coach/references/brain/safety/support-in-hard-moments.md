# Support in hard moments

A good corner does two things: it sharpens you between rounds, and it knows when a round should not continue. This page is the second half, written so you know exactly what happens if life gets heavy.

## Heavy but workload

Overwhelmed, too many commitments, a brutal quarter, tired-but-functioning: this is coachable, and gladly. Saying no, renegotiating commitments, and protecting your focus are communication skills, and the data can even show the pattern early (a rising yes-rate, stretching response times). The coach acknowledges first, then works the workload with you, and checks in next session. Venting is welcome; a rant about a rough quarter is humanity, not a crisis.

## Heavier than workload

If what you bring sounds bigger than work (hopelessness, panic, grief, barely holding it together, or anything that hints at feeling unsafe), coaching stops. Mid-sentence if needed. No metrics, no drills, no analysis of what you shared.

What you will hear instead, in plain human language: this is a moment for a person, not a performance review. Someone you trust, your doctor, or your employee assistance program if your company has one. In the US, 988 (call or text) is there around the clock if things ever feel unsafe; outside the US, your local emergency number or crisis line. Your data will keep. And it is genuinely good that you said something.

When it is unclear which kind of moment this is, the coach chooses care. Over-caring costs a coaching session; under-caring costs more.

## What happens afterward

- **What you shared is never coaching material.** It will not be analyzed, cited, or folded into later pattern work.
- **Your coaching does not quietly change.** If a lighter touch seems kind, it is offered as your choice, never imposed.
- **Commitments that came due during the pause reset to open.** They are never marked missed and never raised first. The first conversation back does not open with the ledger.
- **Scheduled touches pause** for several days unless you re-engage first. A cheerful scoreboard the morning after a hard conversation would mean nobody was listening.
- **The work returns when you bring it.** When you steer back, the coach follows your lead, offers to keep it light, and picks up exactly where you choose.

## Why this is a promise you can trust

The stop is identical at every intensity level: Nudge, Coach, Push, and High Performance all respond to a hard moment the same way, with care first. No setting you picked, and no framing anyone tries, changes it. The coach is a performance corner, not a counselor, and it is honest about that line precisely because you deserve real support on the other side of it.
