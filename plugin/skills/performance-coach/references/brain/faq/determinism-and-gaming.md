# FAQ: Same call, same score (and why gaming does not work)

RapportScore is science-based, AI-enhanced. The measurement layer is deterministic: the same conversation produces the same measurements, every run. Interpretation happens after measurement, and the coach is the interpretation layer, never the author of the numbers.

**What does "deterministic" mean for me?**
Seventeen measurement categories produce 2,400+ data points per recorded call, computed directly from the transcript and timing. Signals like the Signal Stack are pure transcript mechanics: no tone-guessing, no subjective scoring. Re-running an analysis yields the same numbers for the same inputs.

**Can the coach bump my score or my belt?**
No, and not as a policy favor: it has no write access to measurement. It reads scores; it never alters them. Belts are earned through the promotion criteria and recorded automatically (see [[belt-system]]).

**Are there magic words that trick a measure?**
None worth knowing. The measures count real behavior: performing curiosity-tokens without curiosity is polishing the mirror instead of the face. The real behaviors are learnable, and each has a drill (see [[drills-library]]).

**What if I only upload my good calls?**
Your call, but you would be curating your own mirror. Thin, cherry-picked samples get "worth watching" language instead of "pattern" language, so the coaching gets vaguer exactly when you want it sharp. The rough calls are where the money is.

**Can I ask how a measure works?**
Yes, and it is encouraged: a deterministic measure has nothing to hide, and understanding it IS coaching. Every measurement note in this brain explains what is counted and how to read it. The line is falsifying inputs or chasing tokens instead of behavior.

**Do trimmed recordings or re-runs help?**
Trimming removes the tape the coaching runs on; re-running teaches you one thing: determinism. The variable is not the engine, it is the next call.

**Is the interpretation layer deterministic too?**
Narratives and coaching language are generated and can vary in wording; the numbers underneath do not. Every claim the coach makes carries receipts: session, timestamp, count.
