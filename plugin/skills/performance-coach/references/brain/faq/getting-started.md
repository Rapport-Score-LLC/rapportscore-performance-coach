# FAQ: Getting started

**What is the first move?**
Record and upload one real conversation: 7 or more minutes, 2 to 6 people. The coach holds opinions until data exists; its coaching runs on your measurements, not vibes. Details on formats and quality: [[recording-and-processing]].

**What does the quiz do?**
The intake quiz establishes your communication baseline and identifies where analysis can help most. Answer honestly: the more honest your answers, the more accurate your personalized coaching. Your quiz baseline also appears on the Rapport Trends chart, so you can see whether your live calls have caught up to your self-assessment.

**What happens after my first analyzed call?**
You get real measurements immediately: the seven-dimension radar, speaker statistics, timeline, and coaching summary. The coach opens with one strength with receipts and one thing worth watching (never "pattern" from a single session), plus the belt path in one line. You start at [[white-belt]].

**Where do I find things?**
The dashboard has four main tabs: Analytics (trends and insights), Session Management (your conversation history), Team Management (collaboration), and Coaching (practice and lessons). Inside a session: Overview, Detailed, Behavioral, and Deep Dive tabs, with a purple "?" on each card explaining exactly what it measures.

**How fast does coaching get personal?**
First call: real numbers. About 4 sessions: personal baselines begin blending in. About 10: baselines are fully yours and per-user reads are trustworthy (see [[personal-baselines]]). Aim for at least 2 recorded sessions per week to keep the data flowing.

**What should I work on first?**
Ask the coach "what should I work on": it picks ONE behavior by leverage and parks the rest by name (see [[session-types]]). Early wins are usually mechanical: question count, response timing, talk balance.

**What is the loop?**
Measure every recorded conversation, get a prescribed lesson for your weakest dimension, practice it live with Ask Alex roleplay, and progress through belts as the three gates pass ([[belt-system]]). Guided tutorials cover your first upload, reading the analysis, the timeline, the radar, and the coaching system.
