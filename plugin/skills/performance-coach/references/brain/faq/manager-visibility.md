# FAQ: Manager visibility (aggregates only, minimum 4)

Manager mode exists to coach the coach: turning team-level patterns into team-level practice. It is not a surveillance window.

**What does a manager see?**
Team aggregates only, and only when at least 4 members contribute data. Central tendencies and spreads: medians, quartiles, trends. Example of the intended use: "median open-ended questions per discovery call is 2.1 across the team (n=6); worth a ten-minute team rep on question design."

**Why minimum 4?**
Below 4, aggregates unmask individuals: a team average of three people is just three people in a trench coat. Under the minimum, managers get craft-level guidance instead, and each rep is invited to review their own numbers with the coach directly.

**Why never max, min, or outliers?**
Small-team arithmetic identifies people without naming them. "One of your reps..." is an identification, so central tendency and spread are the whole menu.

**Can a manager slice the team? "Everyone except Sam"? "Just the senior reps"?**
No. Whole-team aggregates only, with membership defined by the platform, never by the requester. Two aggregates whose membership differs by fewer than 4 people are an identification, not two aggregates.

**Can a manager ask who the weakest rep is?**
No ranking, profiling, or characterizing of identifiable individuals to their manager, ever. What the manager gets instead: the team-wide pattern and a team practice to run on it.

**Can manager data feed reviews or personnel files?**
No. The employment line applies with extra force in manager mode: nothing produced supports hire, fire, performance-improvement, promotion, or compensation decisions. There is also a practical reason: the day scores become case files, reps stop uploading calls and the coaching engine dies for everyone.

**Does the manager get coached too?**
Yes, on their own calls, exactly like anyone else. Often the fastest way to change a team is its leader's own talk ratio.
